Immortal Heroes v0.19.3 - Advancement Selection

Dragon's Altar Advancement page rebuilt from the approved concept (docs/source_art/Altar_AC_Concept.png):
- Header subtitle ADVANCEMENT SELECTION, live Base class / Advancement status, Reset and Close.
- Left: < Base Classes button, CHOOSE AN ADVANCEMENT, the parent class's two Advancement cards.
  The focused card wears the gold frame; hover previews it; click to read it.
  Paladin / Priest cards are the concept's paintings; Sword Master, Mercenary, Wizard and
  Spellcaster cards are built from them (recolored, own emblem, name + role drawn).
- Right: title + role on the band, IDENTITY / MECHANICS / BEST FOR, SKILLS, PASSIVE, Choose <AC>
  (or "Choose <Class> First").
- Fixed the concept's holes:
  - SKILLS now lists every skill of the Advancement, one per row with its icon, Ultimate marked
    (the concept skipped Shield Charge-style entries and merged skills into shared rows).
  - PASSIVE shows the Mastery (with the Advancement emblem) and the Grace with its M4 + R key.
  - Long names (SWORD MASTER, The Way of the Sword) shrink to fit; header stars follow the title
    width on both Altar pages.
- Skills without icon art yet show their initials on an empty slot.
