Aethelborn Ascended v0.25.243 - First Cast Sounds   (baseline v0.25.242; Anchor stays v0.25.238)
User: stutter on the first cast of skills again since the SFX work.

Cause (found in the sound engine): each skill sound is prepared in the background after load (a processed copy with its
speed / bass / volume baked in). But a cast asked for a copy at the exact share of that hit - barrage hits at 40%,
per-wave sharing, layered companions, curved barrage cuts. Every share the warm-up had not made was built DURING the cast
(read the whole recording, limiter, time-stretch) on the main thread = the first-cast stutter. It came back with the SFX
because the Warrior-bloodline sounds are the ones with speed/bass/volume shaping.

Fix: a cast always uses the copy the warm-up built (full component level); the hit's share is applied as plain volume,
which costs nothing. Same loudness at default settings; very quiet shared hits of an above-100% component may sound a
touch more compressed (the limiter now acts once, on the full level).
Proof in the log:
 - "[Aethelborn] Skill sounds warmed: N processed copies ready." about 20 s after you load in (stand idle once).
 - "[Aethelborn] Sound copy built during a cast (not warmed yet): <cue>" if any copy is still built mid-cast (max 3 lines).
Affects every Warrior / Sword Master / Mercenary skill sound (Normal and Ascended alike); no sound, volume default,
VFX or gameplay changes otherwise. Cleric/Sorcerer/Ranger sounds unaffected (they do not use processed copies).
