IMMORTAL HEROES - ANIMATION STUDIO (press F9 in a world)
=========================================================

WHAT IT IS
A posing tool for the Warrior / Sword Master / Mercenary skill animations.
LEFT = pick a skill. MIDDLE = a safe copy of your character. RIGHT = frames + buttons.
Nothing changes in your real game until you press APPLY TO GAME.

5 STEPS
1) LEFT: click a skill (example: "Circle Swing - Crow hop + spin").
2) RIGHT: click a FRAME button.
   "Wind-up" = before the hit, "HIT" = the strike moment, "After" = follow-through.
3) MIDDLE: drag a coloured DOT on your character (17 dots):
     RED    right shoulder / elbow / hand      BLUE   left shoulder / elbow / hand
     WHITE  the weapon in each hand (dual wield and shields too) - the real weapon follows it
     GREEN  head, chest, torso, hips          PINK   whole body (dot between the feet)
     YELLOW knees (where they point) and ankles (move the foot; drag up = lift it)
   With a two-hand grip: the blue hand slides along the handle, the blue elbow still moves.
   Hover a dot to see what it does. The line under the frames shows its numbers live.
   The faint rings = BODY OUTLINE. With "Block moves that go inside the body" on, any move that
   pushes an arm or hand into the torso/head is undone (rings turn red + message).
   POSE PRESETS: Two hands on one weapon, Two-hand overhead, Let go of the 2nd hand, Guard,
   Dual wield ready, Mirror right -> left, Arms relaxed, Wide planted stance, Legs normal,
   Copy / Paste frame. They set the selected frame; every dot still works on top.
4) PLAY PREVIEW plays the whole motion in a loop. STOP to pose again.
5) APPLY TO GAME saves it and your character uses it in combat right away.

OTHER BUTTONS
UNDO = step back.  RESET = back to how it was when you opened this skill.
REVERT TO ORIGINAL = delete your version, the built-in animation comes back (a .bak copy is kept).
LOAD MY LAST CAST = use the skill once in the game first, then this loads exactly what played.
EXACT NUMBERS = the same values as the dots, typed or slid (optional). Changes show instantly.
"Use my APPLIED animations in the game" = master switch (off = originals everywhere).

LIMITS
- Skills that use Valheim's own swing (e.g. Moonlight Splitter) only let you pose ON TOP of it.
- Hands are clamped so the arm never locks straight; head/chest turns are clamped to human limits,
  but the studio cannot detect clipping by itself - check from the FRONT and SIDE views.
- Files: BepInEx/config/ImmortalHeroesAnimationPresets/<animation>.json
