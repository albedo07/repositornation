Immortal Heroes v0.25.42 - Weapons and Chains
=============================================
NORMAL ATTACK CHAINS
- Real 1-2-1-2-3: the first time each swing of a weapon plays, the game remembers which animation it is;
  the repeated swings (hits 3 and 4) then blend straight into swing 1 and 2, hit 5 is the finisher.
  (Valheim's animator can only chain 1 -> 2 -> 3 by itself, which is why the old remap looked wrong.)
- Dual wield: copy DualWield.dll (the DualWield mod by Smoothbrain, from Thunderstore) into
  BepInEx/plugins/ImmortalHeroesAssets/ - NOT into plugins. Immortal Heroes reads its dual-wield animations from it
  and uses them while you hold two one-handed weapons (same clip map as the mod). Without the DLL the
  off-hand still strikes every swing. Log line: "[Immortal Heroes] DualWield animations loaded".
SKILLS NEED THEIR WEAPON (Graces excepted)
- Warrior / Mercenary: melee weapon - Sword Master: Sword - Cleric / Paladin / Priest: Mace/Club or Staff/Wand -
  Sorcerer / Archmage / Horizon Walker: Staff or Wand - Ranger / Bowmaster: Bow or Crossbow - Acrobat: Bow.
ANIMATIONS
- Whirlwind: Circle Swing's spin looped continuously (the spin part repeats, no wind up / recovery between turns).
  [Runtime] WhirlwindLoopStart_v02542 / WhirlwindLoopEnd_v02542 tune the loop points.
- Shield Charge Hammer Slam: the Axe heavy attack.
- Frenzied Charge: the Sword heavy attack, its pose held for the whole dash; the dash is now near-instant
  (0.12 s), then a hail of slashes follows the path: one every 0.2 s, 1 m apart, each hits everything within 1 m.
- Sky Summon skills (Righteous Strike, Goddess Relic, Relics, Heaven's Judgement, Tempest, Glacial Descent):
  the Impact Wave animation.
- Blade Storm: the sheathed blade now rests outside the body (no more sword through the hip).
- Battlecry roars while moving too (stops you for the wind up).
ART
- Cleric and Sorcerer (and their Advancements) skill icons fill the whole frame: the flat navy matte around the
  art-pack paintings is trimmed before fitting (tree canvases, hotbar icons, Altar icons).
