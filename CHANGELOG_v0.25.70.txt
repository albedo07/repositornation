Immortal Heroes v0.25.70 - Bat Swing

- Olympic Hero (Electric Smite / Bonecrusher jump slam): the off hand now braces in front of the chest for the whole jump. That is the shield held up, or the Mercenary's off-hand weapon held close, instead of hanging out to the side. This includes the landing smash.
- Circle Swing: the crow hop is now a violent baseball-bat load.
  - Both hands grip the weapon together, cocked at the right shoulder through the hop.
  - The two-handed grip carries on as you uncoil into the spin.
  - The off-hand item (shield or second weapon) is hidden while both hands are on the weapon.
  - The v0.25.69 shield brace on the hop is replaced.
