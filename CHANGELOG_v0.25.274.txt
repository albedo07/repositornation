  bosses), added in v0.25.263 but never written into the text.

LEFTOVER CODE REMOVED / SKIPPED

  Judgement Hammer swing, the Shield Charge BigWhoosh climax, Moonlight afterimage, the old Battlecry cry and the seven
  Mercenary rumbles. Nothing plays them; they were costing warm-up time and memory. Their F8 sliders were already hidden.

SWING TIMING (borrowed Valheim swings, every class)
- Your last log measured the swing's hit frame against the skill's hit: Shield Charge hammer slam 0.36 s late (every
  cast), Divine Intervention 0.40 s late, Grand Cross second swing 0.15 s late, Righteous Strike 0.20 s late on its
  first cast, Lightning Tempest 0.89 s early.
- Now every cast measures that difference and keeps a correction per animation (70% of the error each cast, at most
  1.5 s, saved between sessions). The next cast sets its swing speed so the hit frame lands that much earlier / later.
  It settles within a few casts.
- Only the animation speed changes (still limited to 0.25x - 4x). Skill timing, hits, damage and sounds do not move.
- Limit: the Normal Shield Charge slam is instant (0.06 s); if even 4x cannot reach the hit frame, it gets as close as 4x
  allows.
- Log: "CA timing <animation> #n: ... (speed Kx, correction was A s, next cast B s)" for the first 3 casts of each.

Not changed (your call): the old Sword Master Grace and Priest Grace footer paintings; the legacy F8 settings.
CHECKS: all ten modules compile (C# 5) with the installer's reference set. Not tested in game.
