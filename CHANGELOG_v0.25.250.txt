WHIRLWIND CA (Normal and Ascended) - new approach
- A new step runs LAST every frame (after the game's animation, the skill animation and the arm guard) and turns the
  held weapon itself: the point of the weapon farthest from its grip is put straight ahead of the body, level.
  It does not depend on the arms or the hand aiming. Main hand and off-hand weapons both; shields/torches/bows left as held.
- The tip is found from the weapon's actual model points (Therzie skinned models included); if a model cannot be read,
  from its model box. Measured once per equipped weapon.
- The weapons' normal rotation is restored the moment the spin ends.
- Log (always written): "[Aethelborn] Whirlwind blade: <item> (right/left hand) tip ... via vertices|mesh box"
  and once per spin "[Aethelborn] Whirlwind arm: hand-shoulder X m of arm Y m; clip driver playing/NOT playing".
- No damage, timing, VFX or sound change.
