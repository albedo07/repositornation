Aethelborn Ascended v0.25.271 - Menu Music Replacement   (base: v0.25.270; ANCHOR v0.25.257)

MAIN MENU MUSIC - REPLACES VALHEIM'S MENU MUSIC (your correction)
- Before: when our music stopped, the mod unmuted Valheim's music source, which was still playing the vanilla menu track,
  so the vanilla menu music came back. That was wrong: MenuMusic replaces the vanilla menu track; it does not sit on top
  of it.
- Now MenuMusic plays exactly when Valheim's own "menu" track plays, and follows Valheim's own volume curve:
  - it fades in the way Valheim's menu music fades in;
  - clicking Play / the character and world screens / the first loading screen: it keeps playing (no early fade);
  - when the game world starts loading, Valheim fades its menu track out - MenuMusic fades out with exactly that fade;
  - when Valheim's fade reaches silence and Valheim moves on to the in-game music, MenuMusic ends (already silent), and
    only then is Valheim's music source unmuted - for the in-game music, never for the vanilla menu track.
- Logging out back to the main menu starts MenuMusic again the same way.
- [Main Menu] Use AA Main Menu = false still fades MenuMusic out (2.5 s) and gives you the vanilla menu music, as that
  option asks for.
- Log lines: "[AA Menu] menu music follows Valheim's menu track", "world loading: menu music fading out with Valheim's
  fade", "menu music faded out with Valheim's own fade; in-game music continues".

No skill, sound, VFX or animation changes. All v0.25.270 changes are included.
CHECKS: all ten modules compile (C# 5) with the installer's reference set. Not tested in game.
