Aethelborn Ascended v0.25.205 - Sorcerer Polish (on the v0.25.204 anchor)

1. METEOR FALL
- The meteor is a real lit boulder:
  - a smooth rock shape with lumps, ridges and crater dents;
  - a pitted basalt texture;
  - glowing lava cracks in its emission;
  - flames peeling off it and streaming behind.
  Before, it was a flat, unlit silver faceted shape.
- Ascended small meteors land with AIR shockwaves (two rings in the air), flames, dirt and debris. Their ground rings
  and landing markers are gone.
- Charge guide works like Elemental Cataclysm: the outer ring shows the full attack radius and a second ring fills it
  as the charge builds.
2. GRAVITY DOMINION + GRAVITY BLAST (user ref): solid black ball, strong white rim and a ragged purple outline, all
   facing the camera so they always show whole. Three tilted 3D orbit rings (real tubes with thickness, brighter
   toward their heads) circle the ball, so they are visible from any angle. They replace the flat disks that went
   edge-on.
3. ELEMENTAL CATACLYSM: a big air bullet crashes down from the sky with a long trail. Fire, frost, lightning and earth
   (a tumbling rock) spiral around it, leaving helical streams; lightning arcs flicker between it and its core.
   - On the ground: one big explosion dome, branching cracks, an air shockwave and a dirt burst.
   - TIMING: the blast now lands when the bullet does, 0.5 s after release. The Ascended second blast keeps its
     delay after the first.
4. FROST GROUND (Frost Nova, Glacial Descent, Cataclysm): a plain frost circle with subtle faded spots. The spiked,
   cogwheel-like rim is gone.
5. ASTRAL GREATBLADE: denser. A near-opaque deep astral core blade inside a thicker body that is 90% opaque instead
   of 72%.
6. GLACIAL DESCENT (normal + Ascended): a crystal with depth:
   - a lit glossy ice core inside a translucent faceted shell;
   - 4 smaller crystals growing off it;
   - star glints flashing over the facets.
7. CHARGED STAFF (Archmage):
   - Charging now carries the staff's own attack movement penalty (its vanilla speed factor), and you can't sprint
     while charging.
   - The impact is a purple explosion dome with purple flames (Staff of Embers style, bigger) plus embers.
8. FIRE EVERYWHERE (ground fire, Flame Burst, fire blasts, burning projectiles, meteors):
   - The old flame texture was a straight-sided triangle, randomly tilted and dimmed by half.
   - Now: a 16-frame animated flame flipbook of upright licking tongues with the colour built in, full brightness and
     turbulence. Preview: docs/previews/PREVIEW_v0.25.205_Flames.png.
   - Ground fire is denser and taller, with a hot glow bed and smoke.
9. FLAME BURST = a flamethrower: a white-hot jet that billows into a rolling, swelling roar of flame, flung sparks,
   heat smoke and a scorch.
10. CLOCKWORK: the Skill Damage bonus is removed (new key SkillDamagePercent_v025205 = 0) and the duration is 45 s
    (new key Duration_v025205). The 50% cooldown reduction is unchanged. Tooltip, description and buff text are
    updated.
