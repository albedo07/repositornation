CHANGES PER SKILL
- Moonlight Splitter (Normal): vanilla wind removed. Each GT now carries a looping Twinkle (pitch 0.85, "big wave") that
  moves with the GT and stops when it ends. Slash + SwordSlash unchanged.
- Moonlight Splitter (Ascended): same on every wave and on the final GT (pitch 0.85). The fast afterimage stays silent.

- Twinkle.wav: your Twinkle.mp3, 0.25-1.95 s, end cross-faded into the start (0.5 s, equal power) -> 1.2 s seamless loop.

- Heavy Slash (Ascended) + Circle Swing + Halfmoon: the battleaxe swing timings are read from the animator right after load.
  The first cast used a 0.85 s guess for a 1.39 s swing - that is why the first Ascended Heavy Slash moved slow.
