IMMORTAL HEROES v0.25.1 - HUD Style
===================================
Immortal HUD (your reference style)
- One dark panel around the item hotbar: "Name · CLASS" on top, then HP, STA + EIT (label, thin bar,
  current / max), Lv + EXP bar (placeholder 0% until XP exists), then our buffs (timer under each).
- Only Immortal Heroes buffs are shown on it; vanilla / other mods' effects stay in the vanilla list
  top right (ours are removed from that list).
- Food is no longer in our panel (vanilla food icons stay). The vanilla health bar is removed.
- Skill hotbar title shows just your current class (no "Class > AC", no level).

Animations
- Crescent Cleave = Sword Pull (blade pulled back at the side, then let go); Halfmoon Slash = Charge
  Release (long held pull, then a big release). The spin is only on the Ascended Halfmoon finisher wave.
- Animation legend added to the Framework.

Altar cards: label veil 12% (was 30%) for every class; Ranger portraits only a hint of green.
Settings reset to defaults once (Dev Mode OFF).
