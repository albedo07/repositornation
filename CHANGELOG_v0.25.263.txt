TWO-HANDED SWORD CA
- The v0.25.260 trick that switched the animator to the battleaxe stance during skills is removed: with a greatsword or
  scythe you keep the greatsword stance. The v0.25.262 speed fix stays, so the swings keep the same speed as other weapons.

SOUND LENGTH = FADE (every skill sound)
- A sound shortened with its Length slider, or one with a set play time (below), now fades out gradually instead of being
  cut: full volume for the first 40%, then turned down smoothly to silence (like a volume knob turned slowly).
- Sounds that end early (an interrupted wind-up, a projectile that disappears, a looping bed at its end) also fade out
  (0.35 s) instead of stopping dead.

MERCENARY
- Battlecry (Grace): plays Valheim's own Forsaken power activation sound for 1.5 s (fading out). The roar now also STUNS
  small and big enemies around you (same radius as the buff), no damage; bosses are never stunned.
