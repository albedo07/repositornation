1. SHARED RULES (approved)
- D = one normal hit of that skill at the same Tier/stats; B = full normal cast where
  named. Ascended % replace any generic Ascension multiplier. Secondary damage uses the
  base value, not a previous hit's final damage. Defenses apply once per event.
- Bosses: immune to Stun, Freeze, roots, knockdown, launch, forced animations. Slows at
  50% strength, max 30%. Pulls at 20%, never snapped/pinned. Damage always applies. Overrides every Framework "Stuns all / Boss" line.
- Big pulls 40% where a skill allows Big.
- One hit per ENEMY (not collider) per permitted event. Repeats don't restart
  stagger/launch. Sub-hits, afterimages, burns, ticks never spawn themselves and never
  multiply Fury / Overcharge gains beyond the normal cast's allowance.
- Stacking burns: tick 0.5s, max 5 stacks per caster per skill per target, refresh
  extends duration without resetting the tick, recast refreshes the same pool.
- Damaging projectiles stop at solid terrain unless the skill is Ghost (Framework).
- Ground effects follow valid surfaces, never through floors/walls.
- Keep friendly-fire / structure rules.
