Immortal Heroes v0.25.44 - Chain Fix

- Normal melee chain rebuilt: our own hit counter forces every swing (like GooCombatOverhaul does):
  1st > 2nd > 1st > 2nd > 3rd for every melee weapon (dual wield too). The 3rd swing is the finisher
  (Valheim's last-hit bonus), then 1 s lockout. Next attack within 0.4 s of a swing continues the chain
  ([Combat] ComboContinueWindow_v02544). The backwards step (2nd -> 1st) cross-fades into the 1st swing state.
- Whirlwind (normal + Ascended): own continuous two-handed spin modelled on the heavy atgeir sweep
  (blade level at waist height, both hands, low hips, forward lean); the vanilla loop is gone.
- Ascended Whirlwind tornado: drags EVERY archetype (Small, Big, Boss) in, 8 m radius, 5 m/s
  (position-driven, stops just inside the blades). Keys *_v02544.
- Frenzied Charge: the sword heavy attack starts the moment the skill is clicked; its speed is scaled
  so the strike lands exactly when the dash begins.
