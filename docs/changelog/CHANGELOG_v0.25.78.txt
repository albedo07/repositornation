Immortal Heroes v0.25.78 - Holy Bulwark

PALADIN
- Ray of Hope (every version): removes every debuff from you and each ally it touches, then grants debuff immunity for 12 s.
  - Covered debuffs: Burning, Poison, Frost, Shock, Spirit Burn, Wet, Smoked, Tar, Slow, Stun, Expose, Cripple.
  - Shows as a new "Purity" buff icon.
- Ascended Ray of Hope: the cleanse moved to the normal version; Ascended now adds:
  - Fire Burn + Spirit Burn on every enemy in the wave for 8 s (20/s each). These stack harder than normal burns: +25% per tick on top of the usual burn ramp.
  - Hope's Mending: allies regenerate 7% of their Max HP every second for 6 s.
  - The 150 HP Barrier stays.
- Holy Bulwark (added to Heaven's Will, the Paladin Mastery):
  - With a Tower Shield your block covers 3x the area: hits from the front and the sides are blocked (300 degrees, only directly behind you gets through).
  - While you block with a Tower Shield, a white-gold holy force field (2 m) curves around your front, pulses softly and flashes on every hit it catches.

BUFF / DEBUFF TOOLTIPS (hover the icons)
- Every buff now lists everything it does:
  - Battlecry: a second icon, "Battlecry - Labor", shows the +25% damage to trees, logs, rocks and ore for 3 min. The creature buff says it only applies to hits on creatures.
  - Knight's Guidance: -30% Stamina use.
  - Heaven's Light: no equipment movement penalty.
  - Tailwind: +30 Jump, no equipment movement penalty, no fall damage.
  - Bless Thy Sinners recovery: -70% Stamina use, 50% Max HP recovered over 6 s.
  - Buckler Parry: your next damaging skill deals +35%.
  - Unchained Fury: every Mercenary skill uses its Ascended version.
  - Purity and Hope's Mending: new icons with their full effects.
