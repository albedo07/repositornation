Immortal Heroes v0.25.55 - Warrior VFX

FIXES
- Hammer Slam (Shield Charge finisher): you are free 0.15s after the hit and the rest of the heavy swing is
  rushed - no more standing locked after the skill is done.
- Normal attack chain: every swing (hits 1-4) now takes the same time, [Combat] ComboSwingSeconds_v02555 =
  0.8s with no attack-speed bonus; class attack-speed bonuses (Sword Master, Mercenary...) shorten it from
  there. The finisher keeps its natural, longest swing. Each swing's natural time is learned live during your
  first full chain with a weapon, so the slow backwards step (2nd -> 1st swing) is evened out from then on.
- A normal attack can no longer follow a skill: skill animations ignore attack events until a new normal attack
  really starts, the last normal attack is cleared when a skill animation starts, and normal attacks can't
  start while a skill is holding you.

WARRIOR VFX (pass 2, same engine as the Cleric)
- Every expanding Warrior ring (Stomp, Bonecrusher, Circle Swing, Eclipse, Halfmoon, Whirlwind sweeps, Seismic
  rupture, Punishing Bomb...) throws a particle shockwave + light; quick tick rings throw sparks.
- Heavy Slash: sparks fly off the arc. Impact Wave: sparks + dust run along the wave. Impact Punch: spark burst.
- Moonlight Splitter waves glow and shed blue light; Crescent Cleave cleaves stream sparks (ember when Ascended).
- Blade Storm cuts flash; Halfmoon arcs spark; Frenzied Charge leaves a blue trail.
- Punishing Bomb: burning fuse, fiery explosion with smoke. Unchained Fury: ember aura + burst.
- Knight's Guidance: pillar of blue light + wave. Battlecry: ember shockwave + burst.
