IMMORTAL HEROES v0.25.6 - ORB AND MOTIONS

HUD
- Stat column 40% longer (HP, STA, EIT bars), frame grows with it.

BARRIER
- Any Barrier (Divine Intervention, Heaven's Crucible, Priest barriers) now wraps the player in a golden,
  bright, see-through orb with a soft gold light. It follows you, pulses, and fades out when the Barrier
  breaks or expires.
- Heaven's Crucible now also shows the Barrier buff icon (HP as stacks).

BUFF ICONS (each effect has its own shape AND colour)
- Redrawn: Hyper Armor = orange anvil (was a shield like Defense), Barrier = gold orb, Defense = steel shield,
  Attack Speed = crossed swords, Clockwork = bronze gear, Overcharge = magenta burst, Fury = flame,
  Hawk's Vigil = hawk eye.
- New per-source icons: Heaven's Light sun, Ray of Hope beams, Knight's Guidance winged boot,
  Battlecry war horn, Holy Relic cross, Divine Intervention wings, Eye of the Storm storm cloud,
  Buckler Parry buckler spark, Bless Thy Sinners heart, Tailwind wind, Phase Flow portal.

ANIMATIONS
- Motions now also play for charged / wind-up skills (charge starting or Stamina/Eitr spent counts as a cast).
- New motions:
  Cleric: Shield Charge (low shield rush + bash), Angel Comet (spring up, head-first dive),
          Divine Intervention + Heaven's Crucible (kneel in prayer).
  Warrior: Impact Punch (shoulder punch), Frenzied Charge (rush), Eclipse (rising spin + crushing cut),
           Stomp (lift + drop), Bonecrusher (uppercut), Seismic Guillotine (leap slam).
  Sorcerer: Flame Burst (flick), Stonefang (crouch + erupt), Meteor Fall (call down the sky),
            Gravity Dominion (pull), Railcannon (recoil), Frost Nova (curl + burst),
            Afterimage Arsenal (flourish), Rift Echo (tear), Gravity Blast (force shove).
  Ranger: Ballista Shot recoil (stronger per stack), Splitting Arrow alternating sweep per volley.

PERF
- HUD Eitr read uses a cached method lookup.
