IMMORTAL HEROES v0.23.4 - HORIZON WALKER
========================================
(First start resets every setting to default once, Dev Mode OFF.)
Includes v0.23.3 Tweaks (docs/changelog/CHANGELOG_v0.23.3.txt).

HORIZON WALKER
- Aiming: Afterimage Arsenal, Rift Echo and the mimics now read the creature under your
  crosshair (its hitbox), so aiming at a Troll hits the Troll, not the ground behind it.
- 16s: Afterimage Arsenal, Rift Echo and Arcane Phalanx (unlaunched swords fade after 16s,
  normal and Ascended).
- Rift Echo: every 0.3s while you hold Left Click, 100% of your attack.
  Ascended: 3 rifts at once, each 100%.
- Afterimage Arsenal: afterimages deal 100% of your attack.
- Ascended Afterimage Arsenal: 3 mimics follow you 0.2s behind - they copy your movement
  (sprint, strafe...), follow your teleports and Rifts, and repeat every weapon attack 0.2s
  later for 100% of your weapon damage. They never cast skills. (Replaces the Astral Clones.)
