Immortal Heroes v0.25.63 - Smash and Crescents

NORMAL ATTACK CHAIN (NACC)
- ROOT CAUSE of the uneven speeds: the chain timer assumed one constant speed for each swing, but Valheim's own
  per-weapon speed changes and the recovery frames made the estimate wrong by a different amount per weapon
  (greatsword vs one-handed sword, dual vs single). It now measures the speed the animation REALLY played at,
  so every weapon lands on the same 0.6 s rhythm (class attack speed on top).
- Dual wield and one-handed swings are timed separately (same trigger names, different clips - sharing them
  made one-handed swings crawl after dual wielding and dual wield too fast at first). Switching between them
  starts a fresh chain. Slowest allowed swing raised (no more ~1 s crawls).

HYPER ARMOR
- Blocking and parrying are covered: Warriors (Sword Master + Mercenary) and anyone with active Hyper Armor
  take no pushback and no slide from a blocked / parried hit.

JUMP SLAMS (Bonecrusher, Electric Smite)
- You always face where you aim in the air; WASD only steers, it never turns you.
- Both arms flare out wide in the air.
- Landing = a real smash: Valheim's axe heavy attack fires just before touchdown so the weapon hits the ground on
  contact, with a deep hero kneel (hips dropped, wide stance). No more fist tap / hidden weapon.

STOMP
- Knee raised past the hip, both arms flared out and up like the roar emote, then the foot is driven down with the
  chest thrown forward and arms flung wide.

LIGHTNING TRAILS (Electric Smite, Ascended Righteous Strike)
- Each trail is now a short (~1.2 m) bolt crawling along the ground; the tail is cut behind it instead of one
  continuous line from the impact. Thinner glow, tighter jitter, fizzles out at the end.

SIGNATURE / SLASH VFX
- Moonlight Splitter / Halfmoon waves: thin moon-crescent blades (needle tips, white-hot core, soft glow), like the
  Dragon Nest magic slash reference.
- Crescent Cleave: the crescent's centre rides at ground level - half of it is underground, it tears up out of the
  earth with rocks wrenched up on both sides as it travels.
- Slash ribbons are crisper (sharp bright edge, faint wake).
- Placeholders removed while the effects are on: every old line ring (Warrior, Cleric, Sorcerer, Ranger rings
  become real shockwaves, spark bursts or textured glowing circles on the ground), the straight bar under
  Moonlight / Halfmoon, the line crescent under Crescent Cleave, the line arcs under slashes, Blade Storm's line
  circles, Frenzied Charge's flat cuts, arcane beam lines and Ranger arrow lines (all glowing streaks now).
