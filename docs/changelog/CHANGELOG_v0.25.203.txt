Aethelborn Ascended v0.25.203 - Solid Objects (visuals only)

1. RELICS + JUDGEMENT HAMMER = plain SOLID objects
- Their material is now Unity's Standard surface, forced fully opaque: it writes depth, draws in the normal geometry
  queue and shows only outer faces.
- The copied vanilla sword material from v0.25.195 is now only a fallback if Standard is missing. It carried hidden
  render settings, and those let inner parts and the far side show through the gold: the hollow, "still loading" look
  and the gems missing from the front.
- The meshes themselves were verified closed and outward-facing in v0.25.202.

2. JUDGEMENT HAMMER HANDLE
- The haft is round (16 sides). It used to be a flattened ellipse, built for the old stretched scaling, which made the
  bands look like plates.
- The grip ring, upper ring and socket collar are real rings, about 1.45x the haft's thickness, with rounded edges,
  all the way around.
- The collar no longer gets squashed by the head reshape.

3. GROUND CRACKS (every skill: Cracks, CrackLine, BranchCrackLine, Shatter, Furrow, Eruption...)
- Each crack is now a flat MESH strip lying on the ground, visible from both faces.
- No LineRenderer anymore, so nothing depends on where the camera looks.

4. BLADE STORM (Judgement Cut, user reference)
- A ball of light: a soft glowing core, white at the heart with a tinted halo and a little blue mist.
- 5 long curved slashes (200-290 deg arcs) sweep around it on different tilted planes. Each is drawn on in 0.07 s,
  staggered 0.05 s apart, then fades.
- Replaces the straight cut lines, crescent flashes and hollow smoke shell.
