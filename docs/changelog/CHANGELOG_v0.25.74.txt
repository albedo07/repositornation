Immortal Heroes v0.25.74 - Bar Labels

HUD (all classes)
- The painted icon + label column next to the bars (HP / Stamina / EXP) is removed from every frame.
  - It no longer fit now that Stamina and Eitr share one bar line.
- The bars now start right next to the medallion, so they are longer.
- Each bar shows its name inside, on the left: HP, STAMINA, EITR, EXP.
  - Cleric, Sorcerer and Ranger: the number sits on the right of the same bar or half. The EXP bar shows your level.
  - Warrior keeps its painted number column on the right.
