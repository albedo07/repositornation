SHARED ASSET FAMILIES (DragonVfx)
- Slash ribbons: magic crescents (Moonlight / Crescent / Halfmoon / Eclipse / Grand Cross) are now a cupped 3-row ribbon
  with a broken, flowing wind texture (scrolls along the blade), one hairline cutting edge on the leading side and
  feathered wakes. Weapon wakes (SlashArc) = broken translucent shoulder + hairline edge. No opaque crescent cut-outs.
- Judgement Cut volume (Blade Storm): 7 curved cut ribbons on random tilted planes (hairline edge + silver/indigo
  shoulder, frayed ends), depth-tested so rear arcs pass behind the victim, 4 interior hairline cuts, faint smoky air,
  arcs snap in inside the hit window, shear along themselves and dissolve into directional threads. No sphere shell.
- Relic materials: pale weathered stone, aged bronze (slight patina), narrow emissive ivory inlay; shared + cached.
- Ground impacts: the expanding ring is now a broken dust pressure front in the ground colour (no glowing ring / disc);
  shockwave streaks are pale air, not neon; contact lights capped small.
- Healing / blessing pulse (SoftWave): low translucent wave of soft strands + faint mist + rising motes (no dirt, no
  flash). Heal ticks: small upward motes, no light per tick.
- Sky beams: light drifts DOWN the beam, fewer streaks, small contact light; compact contact bloom.
- Gravity: dark partly transparent core, narrow muted bent rim (was wide purple disks), curved inward strands
  (orbital), translucent warped-air wisps (no ring billboards), loose soil that lifts and curls inward.
- Fire: compact hot core, low fast-decaying smoke (no tall column), flame cones with dark smoke pockets and gaps.
- Per-tick rings removed (the contact / hit-impact layer carries ticks); full-duration area circles are guides.
