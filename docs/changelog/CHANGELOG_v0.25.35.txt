Immortal Heroes v0.25.35 - Swing Timing
=======================================
- Vanilla-based skill animations now play in two phases: the wind up (draw back) is slowed down to fill the
  skill's wind up, then the swing itself plays fast in the last ~0.25 s so the weapon connects exactly when
  the skill hits. The follow-through after the hit is finished quickly.
- Moonlight Splitter: the wind up IS the first slash (slow draw, fast cut) - no more frozen raised-sword pose.
  Slashes 2 and 3 start 0.3 s early so each cut lands on its wave (same 0.5 s spacing). Ascended too.
- Halfmoon Slash: same animation as Heavy Slash.
- Crescent Cleave: same animation as Impact Wave. Ascended Crescent Cleave: Valheim's leaping sword special.
- Frenzied Charge: sword held out in front, point forward, for the whole charge (running legs underneath).
- Circle Swing: no more steering during the wind up; the crow hop now carries you 1.5 m forward (walls stop it).
