AETHELBORN ASCENDED v0.25.196 - HOLLOW CLOUDS AND RELIC DETAIL
Visual revision pass based on the original v195 archive. Not an Anchor promotion.

1. BLADE STORM
- Cloud patches now curve around a spherical surface and leave the center empty.
- Small surface wisps soften its edges. No filled fog volume or solid barrier mesh.
- Shell mesh, textures and materials are cached and prepared before casts.
- Attack slashes, damage, targets, timing and hit area are unchanged.

2. GODDESS RELIC, JUDGEMENT HAMMER AND PRIEST RELICS
- Warm gold bodies and ivory details use colored albedo textures with grain and veins, rather than relying on a white texture multiplied by material tint.
- Blue and healing-green gems have their own colored, faceted texture. Cross gems are mounted on the visible front/back faces instead of buried inside the body.
- Hammer head is thicker, striking faces retain ivory detail, and its larger blue gems are more exposed. Grip and gameplay size are unchanged.
- Priest relics share these material fixes while retaining their lightning/healing identities, healing heart and pulses.

3. ASCENDED HEAVY SWING / HEAVY SLASH
- The three visual strokes use their explicit intended planes. Stale motion sampled from strike two can no longer turn the third downward slash into a horizontal effect.
- The existing three strikes, native animations and strike interval are unchanged.

4. DAMAGING SUPPORT SKILLS
- Added brief, restrained radius mist to Ray of Hope and Divine Intervention, plus Ascended Lightning Relic on landing.
- No additional damage, healing, pulses, duration or resource cost.

5. HEAVEN'S JUDGEMENT
- Radius guide is now a double-ring sigil with twelve rune marks, spokes and a central cross/diamond.
- Follows the ground, stays within the existing radius, lasts through the existing cast/barrage and honors the guide toggle.

SCOPE
Only CombatRuntime and Advanced source modules changed, plus release banner text and documentation. All eight other source modules, 185 existing art/animation assets and configuration defaults are unchanged. Extra skill point lights remain removed. Final shader appearance and performance require in-game testing.
