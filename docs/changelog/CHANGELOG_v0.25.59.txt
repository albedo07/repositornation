Immortal Heroes v0.25.59 - Flowing Chains (normal attack chain + dual wield fixes, VFX skin pass)

NORMAL ATTACK COMBO CHAIN (NACC)
- No more hit-stop: Valheim froze your swing for 0.15 s on EVERY hit (that was the "stuck in the enemy" feel).
  Your melee swings now flow straight through enemies. [Combat] MeleeHitStop_v02559 = on brings it back.
- Interval: 0.6 s per swing for all 5 hits, finisher included ([Combat] ComboSwingSeconds_v02559); class attack
  speed bonuses still make it faster. Swing lengths are measured per hit (the finisher start -> end).
- The pause between hit 2 and hit 3 is gone: when the chain steps back to swing 1 it now cross-fades into it
  22% in (past its rest-pose wind up) so it flows out of swing 2 ([Combat] ComboRepeatOffset_v02559,
  ComboBlendSeconds_v02559).

DUAL WIELD (Mercenary)
- Root cause of the extra clunk: every hit froze the swing twice (main + off-hand hit) - gone with the hit-stop fix.
- The dual-wield animation set is now swapped in/out between attacks (on equip), never inside a swing trigger
  (swapping mid-chain reset the animator).
- Off-hand hit lands on the first target it meets (like the DualWield mod); off-hand weapon trail now shows.

VFX - TONED DOWN + SKIN
- Flashes / lights at 45% ([Visuals] LightIntensity_v02559), particle density 0.7 ([Visuals] ParticleDensity_v02559),
  glow particles slightly softer.
- New skin layer (generated textures, no asset files):
  - Glowing ground rings painted on the terrain under every shockwave + a soft glow disc.
  - Scorched / broken ground decal that lingers after heavy hits, rolling smoke puffs.
  - Solid light columns (crossed textured planes) inside every pillar (crosses, Knight's Guidance, Clockwork, sky strikes...).
  - Slash ribbons: a crescent of light sweeps left -> right with each slash (Heavy Slash, Halfmoon / Circle Swing,
    Crescent Cleave overhead cut, Judgement Cut, Frenzied hail, Eclipse rings, Whirlwind, Astral Greatblade chop,
    Flame Burst).
  - Dust and clouds are soft smoke puffs instead of glow dots; feathers are real feather shapes that tumble.
