IMMORTAL HEROES v0.25.23 - STORYBOARD REBUILD (built only from the storyboards + the user's animation guide)

Why the old poses looked wrong: every arm pose was a guessed bone rotation stacked on top of Valheim's own animation,
so the hand never landed where the storyboard puts it.
New engine:
- HAND TARGETS: each storyboard frame places the main hand at an exact spot relative to the shoulder (straight up,
  out in front, at the left shoulder, behind the shoulder, by the ribs, down at the ground...) and arm IK puts it
  there, whatever the rig's bone axes are. The weapon points where the hand points; two-handed moves put the off hand
  on the grip (its own item hidden while gripping).
- Feet stay planted (scale-correct now); stances are shallower and calmer.
Families (storyboard / guide):
- BDCA (heals, buffs, Battlecry): main hand straight up, weapon up.
- SSCA (relics, strikes, Glacial, Meteor, Cataclysm): hand at the chest -> straight up to the sky -> arm out in
  front, weapon pointing at the ground ahead.
- SAA / CSAA / CSAFA (Heavy Slash, Moonlight, Crescent, Blade Storm, Halfmoon): two hands, cocked over the left
  shoulder -> out left -> across the front -> out right (Halfmoon starts from the poke pull at the right hip;
  Ascended finisher spins clockwise).
- GAA (Impact Wave, Seismic): hands at the right shoulder (baseball), U through the ground, finish high left.
- OSA (Astral Greatblade): two hands straight up, straight down.
- JSAA (Electric Smite, Bonecrusher): fist cocked back + crouch, fist forward launch, counter-clockwise roll, fist down,
  fist on the ground with the left foot forward and the right knee low, recover.
- FTAA (Impact Punch, Frenzied Charge): hand at the ribs -> straight at the target. Shield Charge: shield up, weapon
  tucked behind it, mace whipped up and smashed down at head height.
- PCA (Zap, Flame Burst, Gravity Blast): hand up by the shoulder -> arm straight at the target.
- TAA (Judgement Hammer): hand behind the shoulder, coil, throw forward, follow across. Punishing Bomb: two-handed bat.
- GCA (Stonefang, Stomp, Snare): hand + weapon down at the ground / foot stamp / low placement.
- SPAA (Circle Swing, Eclipse): weapon held level out to the side while turning.
- AFA (Railcannon): two hands on the staff along the aim.  RCA (Horizon Walker): point / open / pinch / pull.
- Angel Comet: arm thrown up on the jump, wings, inverted dive with the arm leading, hero landing.
