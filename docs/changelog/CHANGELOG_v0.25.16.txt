IMMORTAL HEROES v0.25.16 - CASTING FAMILIES (Animation Blueprint, part B)

Every skill below now uses its blueprint family (one shared motion, hand profile per class:
Cleric shield stays close, Sorcerer off hand balances, Ranger bow held low, Warrior off weapon low).

- SKY COMMAND (call-and-lower): Righteous Strike (+Ascended), Goddess Relic, Lightning Relic, Holy Relic,
  Heaven's Judgement, Glacial Descent (+Ascended); Lightning Tempest = both arms reach. Gather (hand across the
  sternum, eyes on the target) -> main arm straight overhead -> pulled down pointing at the target -> guard.
  Meteor Fall / Elemental Cataclysm hold at the CALL pose while charging and release on your release.
- WAVECALLER (gather-and-open): Holy Wave, Ray of Hope (blessing palm), Heaven's Light (protective),
  Divine Intervention (palm toward the source), Heaven's Crucible (barrier forearms), Frost Nova (compressed,
  sharp outward snap), Clockwork (wrist turn), Knight's Guidance / Hawk's Vigil (brow salute into the opening),
  Tailwind. Instant skills now react at once (release-first, 0.06 s) with a short follow-through.
- PULSE DRIVE (chamber-and-push): Lightning Zap / Flame Burst (palm cone), Impact Punch (fist), Ascended Holy
  Wave on an ally (gentle palm), Gravity Blast (two-hand orb push). Lunge legs under the push.
- POWER PITCH: Judgement Hammer = overarm pitch (hips first, follow-through across the body; the catch stays
  its own gesture); Punishing Bomb = sideways bat strike.
- WAR CRY: Battlecry = gather, chest open, roar, settle (no trembling). Unchained Fury and Overcharge are now
  tiny chest/shoulder accents and never interrupt a skill.
- PHOENIX RISE: Bless Thy Sinners' save = short upper-body brace-rise-open accent (no crouch, no lock).
