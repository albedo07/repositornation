IMMORTAL HEROES v0.23.2 - SPELLCASTER
=====================================
(First start resets every setting to default once, Dev Mode OFF.)
Includes v0.23.1 Wizard (see docs/changelog/CHANGELOG_v0.23.1.txt).

SPELLCASTER (Framework 3.2)
- Yin and Yang: any Staff / Wand attacks twice as fast (interval -50%), single or dual.
- Arcane Phalanx: 4 swords (was 8). Holding Mouse1 launches one every 0.3s (hard buffer,
  releasing Mouse1 never resets it). Recast arms the volley, Left Click fires all.
  Cooldown starts once every sword is gone. Damage in F8 "Spellcaster Arcane Phalanx Damage".
- NEW Gravity Blast: Free Aim Laser orb (Greydwarf Brute size), 15m over 4s, Slash + Pierce
  every 0.5s in 5m, passes through enemies, stops at walls, pulls Small slowly, Cripples
  Big/Boss 3s (refreshed per hit).
- NEW Rift Walker (Grace, M4+R): Portal A, then Portal B within 30s, each up to 50m
  (Free Aim, mid-air allowed). Linked for 30s, E to travel either way, airborne exits get
  Feather Falling. 2 min cooldown from Portal A (a failed B still uses it).
- Arcane Rupture damage now in F8 ("Spellcaster Arcane Rupture Damage").
- Tree tooltips show full stats for every Spellcaster skill and Rift Walker.
- Altar: Yin and Yang (Mastery) + Rift Walker (Grace) texts, correct skill list.

ASCENDED (all 7)
- Stonefang Eruption (Ascended MC): 7m, no wind up, eruption 80%, then the spikes stay 4s
  and hit every 0.5s for 20% (8 ticks).
- Arcane Phalanx: 8 swords at 65% each; launching all 8 together erupts an Astral Spear on
  the first impact (4m, 80% of one sword, four spears).
- Afterimage Arsenal: up to 3 Astral Clones, 16s each. Tap = one clone, hold 1s = every
  missing one, at 3 a tap replaces the oldest. Each clone has its own 45s cooldown. Clones
  copy your weapon attacks at 30% (while you hold Mouse1) and your Gravity Blast / Arcane
  Rupture at 25%. They leave on death or class change. (Other skill copies are visual only.)
- Void Step: 2 charges (recovered one after the other), keeps your momentum.
- Rift Echo: echoes every 0.35s instead of 0.5s.
- Gravity Blast: 25m, pulls Big 40% / Bosses 20%, bursts for 130% when the orb stops.
- Arcane Rupture: 4 charges, 120% damage.
