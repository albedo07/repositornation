Immortal Heroes v0.25.30 - Wind Up Timing
=========================================
- Vanilla-based skill animations now START with the wind up and END exactly when the wind up ends.
  The game measures the playing animation every frame and speeds it up or slows it down to fit
  (0.3x - 6x). Short wind ups play it faster, long ones slower. Anything left after the impact is finished at 6x.
  Circle Swing: crow hop first, then the spin fills the rest of the wind up.
- Jump and Slam (Olympic Hero: Bonecrusher / Electric Smite): no more superman pose. The body leans only
  diagonally (~38 deg) in the air, the main arm is flared a little to the side and the weapon is carried up, not pointed.
