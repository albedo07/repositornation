Immortal Heroes v0.25.41 - Emotes and Judgement Cut
===================================================
EMOTE ANIMATIONS (Valheim's own gestures, natural speed, their own legs)
- Battlecry: roar (was challenge).
- Unchained Fury: flex (only when you are standing still; running keeps the run).
- Snare Trap and Stonefang Eruption: kneel (you stand back up when the skill ends).
- Gravity Dominion: come-here beckon (was point).
- You stand still during the wind up of these (at least 0.5 s); Horizon Walker casts and Fury stay mobile.
- If your game lacks an emote, the skill keeps its previous pose.
BLADE STORM = JUDGEMENT CUT (Vergil, Devil May Cry)
- Crouched iai stance with the blade held back at the left hip, a blink-fast draw that ends with the sword arm
  out to the right, a held beat, then the slow sheathe back to the hip and a small settle (the "click").
