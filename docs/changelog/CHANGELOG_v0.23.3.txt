IMMORTAL HEROES v0.23.3 - TWEAKS
================================
(First start resets every setting to default once, Dev Mode OFF.)

GENERAL
- Ruler: Units Per Meter Override default 1.5 (your new measurement).
- Names: Wizard is now ARCHMAGE, Spellcaster is now HORIZON WALKER everywhere you can
  read it (Altar, Skill Tree, HUD, F8 sections). Saved characters keep working.
- Charged skills: the charge timer starts the moment you press the key (the wind up runs
  at the same time): Meteor Fall, Astral Greatblade, Elemental Cataclysm.

FIXES
- HUD title (Mercenary Fury) no longer wraps into two overlapping lines; it has a shadow.
- Altar Advancement page summary box: lighter parchment, title + body as one block centred
  both ways inside it, shrinks to fit so nothing sticks out.

MERCENARY
- Ascended Heavy Slash: 110 Slash (110% of the normal 100).
- Bonecrusher, Circle Swing, Punishing Bomb (and its ground fire): 7m, normal and Ascended.
- Whirlwind: every tick = one normal attack of your weapon (was half).
- Ascended Whirlwind: tornado (rings) that pulls every enemy within 4m (Big 40%, Bosses
  20%); the blades still hit at 2m.
- Stomp: aftershock and the Ascended third shock now deal 100% of the first stomp.
- Punishing Bomb: explosion = 200% of your basic attack damage.
- Seismic Guillotine: 2.0x weapon damage (was 1.2x).

ARCHMAGE
- Charged Staff attack: 1 stack per second (was 1 per 2s).
- Ascended Meteor Fall: 0.5s wind up.
