IMMORTAL HEROES v0.25.14 - SMOOTH MOTIONS (animation polish: no more shaking)

- Engine fix: on frames where Valheim's animator does not rewrite a bone (physics-rate / culled updates), the skill
  offset used to be stacked on top of itself and snap back next frame = jitter. Offsets are now always applied on
  top of the last real animator pose (skill clips and the old pose driver).
- All deliberate trembling removed: Halfmoon charge, Battlecry / Unchained Fury roar, Overcharge, Frost Nova,
  Tailwind, legacy body-motion and pose-driver oscillations.
- Ranger quick shots: the extra body recoil per shot is gone (the vanilla bow animation already recoils).
