IMMORTAL HEROES v0.25.26 - ANATOMY

- ARMS FOLLOW HUMAN ANATOMY: a hand reaching across the body now goes IN FRONT of the chest (never behind the head);
  elbows hang down and slightly out, point down-forward when the hand crosses the chest, up-forward-out when the
  hand is raised to throw / strike, and back when the hand is pulled back (ribs chamber, bowstring).
  Heavy Slash / Moonlight: both hands cocked IN FRONT of the left shoulder, then the sweep to the right.
- STOMP: the weight shifts onto the left leg, the right knee rises to hip height (thigh level, knee bent), then the
  foot is driven flat into the ground and both knees absorb it (new foot-lift support).
- WHIRLWIND / FURIOUS WINDS: a continuous, violent spin (one full turn every ~0.3 s, faster when Ascended) with the
  weapon held out level - no longer a repeated vanilla attack. Circle Swing / Eclipse keep the one-shot vanilla spin.
