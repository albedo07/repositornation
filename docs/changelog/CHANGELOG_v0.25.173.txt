Aethelborn Ascended v0.25.173 - Full Cleave
Baseline: v0.25.172. Only Halfmoon Slash's follow-up slashes changed.

HALFMOON SLASH (user video: follow-up slashes missed a Troll inside the cleave)
- Cause: each follow-up slash only hit a 2 m sphere ON the rim of the arc (at the full Halfmoon radius). Anything standing
  inside the arc - closer than the rim, like a Troll right in front of you - was never touched by them.
- Now every follow-up slash hits EVERYONE inside the cleave: the same 170 deg frontal arch and radius as the opening slash
  (measured from where you cast, collider-aware so big bodies count), plus whatever its own 2 m cut touches at the rim.
  One hit per target per slash. Normal and Ascended (the Ascended keeps the normal follow-up before its stance).
- Per-hit damage (50%), Spirit DoT, stun rules, slash count and timing unchanged. Note: enemies inside the arch now take
  every follow-up slash, so total damage on them is much higher than before (that is the requested behaviour).
- Tooltip / Altar description updated.
