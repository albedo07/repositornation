Immortal Heroes v0.25.109 - Flip Studio
Base: v0.25.108. Only the F9 Animation Studio.

- Crouch (jump prep): ONLY the hips and legs change - hands, chest, head and weapon stay as they are;
  hips drop and sit back, feet planted a little apart, knees bend forward over the toes.
- New ORANGE 'ROTATE / FLIP' dot beside the body: drag in any direction to rotate the whole character
  any way (free trackball around the body centre, even upside down) for acrobatic / gymnastic moves.
- Presets: Flip forward 90, Upside down, Lie on the side, Rotation reset.
- Tip: for a full flip across frames, use the Spin value or several frames (big angle jumps between
  two frames can take the short way round).
