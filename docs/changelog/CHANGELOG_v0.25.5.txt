IMMORTAL HEROES v0.25.5 - HUD Rework
====================================
Why the vanilla HP never went away: Hud.instance is a property, not a field - our lookup returned
nothing, so none of the hiding code ever ran. Fixed (property + field fallbacks).

Stat HUD
- Vanilla health bar ("Health") and its icon ("healthicon") switched off, stamina / eitr bars too, and
  Hud.UpdateHealth / UpdateStamina / UpdateEitr blocked (EpicMMO's method).
- The VANILLA food icons now sit inside our HUD: the vanilla food panel is moved into our frame every
  frame and the ornate frame is a uGUI image placed behind it (like the old hotbar version). Same
  vanilla icons, timers and tooltips; our own food copy is gone.
- Draggable: open the inventory, drag the HUD anywhere; the position is saved ([Immortal HUD] PosX /
  PosY, -1 = default bottom-left).
- Hidden (food included) while the Altar, the Skill Tree, the Esc menu or the big map is open; stays
  visible with the inventory so you can move it.
- Buff timers no longer cropped.
Settings reset to defaults once (Dev Mode OFF).
