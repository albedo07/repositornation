IMMORTAL HEROES v0.25.2 - HUD Fixes
===================================
Immortal HUD
- Its own panel UNDER the vanilla item hotbar; the hotbar is no longer moved or wrapped.
- Reference style: dark panel with a thin grey border, "Name · CLASS" title, HP / STA / EIT bars with
  deep, saturated colours (Linear colour space compensated - no more pink HP), Lv + EXP line, our buffs
  in bordered boxes with a coloured line and the timer under each.
- Hidden while the inventory, a menu or the big map is open (no more drawing over the inventory).
- Vanilla HP / Stamina / Eitr fully removed: every Hud health / stamina / eitr element, including the
  vertical health bar, its number and icon. The vanilla food icons stay.

Performance (lag)
- The HUD searched the scene for the hotbar every frame and re-measured it (now once per second) and
  gathered its data on every IMGUI event (now once per frame).
- The Skill Tree searched all loaded fonts on every draw call (now cached).
- Every class / AC check read the player data through fresh reflection on every hit, speed, stamina
  and regen call (Combat Runtime, Advanced, Skills, Sorcerer, Ranger): now cached.
- Ranger: gear rules 5x per second instead of every frame; ground / attack / draw checks cached.
Settings reset to defaults once (Dev Mode OFF).
