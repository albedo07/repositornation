AETHELBORN ASCENDED v0.25.286 - Sorcerer Sounds

Your SorcererSFX recordings, decoded to WAV with no edits (one trim: the Black Hole "vanish" second, see Gravity
Dominion). 14 new sound files, 29 new sound entries, 12 new audio sections in F8 (each sound its own Volume slider
plus the usual Length / Pitch / Speed / Bass / Sync).

SORCERER (base)
- Flame Burst, Stonefang Eruption: unchanged (your "Flame2" and "Earth").
- Glacial Descent (Normal + Ascended): IceForming plays the moment you cast (during the wind-up); IceCrash lands with
  the ice (its hit lined up with the landing). The old GD_Formation / GD_Impact cues are no longer used.

ARCHMAGE (Wizard)
- Meteor Fall: Flame starts at the cast and loops for as long as you hold the charge (past 3 stacks too), fading out
  on release. The meteor crash = SolidBoom (hit lined up with the landing).
  Ascended: every small meteor = MidBoom.
- Gravity Dominion: BlackHole plays for the whole well (fades in, fades out near the end). The recording's vanishing
  second (0:02-0:01 before its end, cut with a soft fade so it never stops abruptly) is timed so its peak lands on the
  end of the well. Ascended: the end blast = MidBoom + BassDrop, on that same peak.
  (Normal Gravity Dominion has no blast, so it ends with the vanish only.)
- Astral Railcannon: Railcannon during the wind-up, BassDrop on the shot.
  Ascended: Railcannon loops for the whole beam, then BassDrop when it ends.
- Astral Greatblade: MagicSword2 when the blade is conjured, Slam on the slam (hit lined up).
  Ascended: one conjure, a Slam on each of the 3 slams.
- Frost Nova (Normal + Ascended): IceSlam, 1.5 s (gradual fade), its hit on the nova.
- Elemental Cataclysm: PowerUp from the cast, looping while the charge is held; on landing BoomBass + SolidBoom
  together. Ascended: the second blast gets BoomBass + SolidBoom too.

HORIZON WALKER (Spellcaster)
- Arcane Phalanx: MagicSword when the swords appear; SwordSlash on release. Releasing all at once plays ONE
  SwordSlash (0.3 s gap, no stacking). Ascended: the Astral Spears = MagicSword pitched lower (80%).
- Rift Echo: SwordSlash2 at 88% pitch on each echo shot.
- Arcane Phantom (Afterimage Arsenal): SwordSlash2 at 118% pitch when the afterimages fire; Ascended when the twins appear.
- Void Step: DBZ on the teleport.
- Gravity Blast: BlackHole2 rides the orb for as long as it exists; its explosion = MidBoom + BassDrop (same as Gravity
  Dominion).
- Arcane Rupture: BoomBass + SolidBoom on the rupture (hit lined up).
