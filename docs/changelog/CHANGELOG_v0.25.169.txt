Aethelborn Ascended v0.25.169 - Arms and Altar (baseline v0.25.168; ANCHOR v0.25.167 untouched)

1. CAPE (Bonecrusher video)
   Confirmed from the video frames (docs/previews/CAPE_v0.25.168_video_frames.png): while airborne the cape is NOT
   RENDERED at all, then at the landing it appears stretched straight up and whips down. Inertia can lag cloth but cannot
   make it invisible -> the cape's MagicaCloth2 simulation was CULLED (camera/distance culling "Keep" = paused) during the
   leap: its renderer bounds stopped updating, Unity frustum-culled the renderer, the paused cloth never refreshed them,
   and on landing it resumed from the takeoff pose. v0.25.168 only damped inertia, so it could not fix this.
   Fix (DragonClothGuard, only while a skill animation drives you): MagicaCloth camera culling Off + distance culling off,
   SkinnedMeshRenderer.updateWhenOffscreen on (bounds always follow the bones), inertia anchored to the player with
   world/local inertia x0.1; everything restored afterwards (0.4 s blend). If a cape renderer is still seen culled mid-skill
   the cloth is reset once when the skill ends, and one log line reports it. Re-applied for every cast (rescanned per skill).
   Cape never hidden; CA, movement, Rigidbody untouched. LogOutput shows "[Aethelborn] Cape guard: MagicaCloth2 found; ..."
   (which settings were found) and, if it ever happens again, "... culled mid-skill ...".

2. NACC airborne rules
   - Airborne primary rejection and the takeoff reset are gone: AA's NACC starts and continues in the air.
   - Ground contact is ignored (rocks, slopes, steps never reset); an airborne chain continues after landing.
   - A deliberate jump (vanilla "jump" trigger) breaks the running chain: the next swing in the air is strike 1.
   - Taking FALL damage (after fall reductions) breaks continuation; other damage does not (v0.25.169b).
   - Continuation window 0.7 s ([Combat] ComboContinueWindow_v025169). The timer starts when the previous swing has
     FINISHED (Attack.Stop = after its recovery), so slow swings keep the whole window; clicks during a swing queue and
     always continue. No primary within 0.7 s -> the chain resets.
   - Five-hit order 1-2-1-2-3, finisher lockout (never touched by any reset), heavy attacks and skill handoffs unchanged.

3. Weapon restrictions (real equip actions + 0.5 s re-check after class changes; prohibited items are unequipped into the
   inventory, never dropped). Only combat equipment is judged: tools, torches, pickaxes, fishing rods always pass.
   - Warrior / Mercenary: no daggers, fist weapons, ranged or magic weapons. Combat axes allowed (modded too).
   - Sword Master: Swords only.  - Cleric / Paladin / Priest: Staves, Wands, Maces/Clubs only.
   - Sorcerer + ACs, Ranger + ACs: no melee except one dagger, no dual daggers; their ranged / shield rules unchanged.
   - Shields: Warrior, Mercenary, Sword Master, Cleric family may equip any shield; a shield disables The Way of the Sword's
     sword-only bonuses (+Sword, +attack speed, no sword movement penalty).
   - Utility axe: classes barred from combat axes (Sword Master, Cleric, Sorcerer, Ranger families) may hold a genuine
     vanilla chopping axe (prefab whitelist AxeStone/Flint/Bronze/Iron/BlackMetal/JotunBane - modded axes incl. Therzie's
     never match); it chops trees and deals no damage to creatures. Mercenary exempt.
   - Mercenary dual wield: the exact same prefab in both hands (quality may differ); never Spears / War Pikes; native 2HDW
     items stay single paired weapons. Warfreak's dual bonus follows the same rule.
   - Kali (future, not built): daggers + fist weapons, dual daggers from the Base Class, bows only with no arrows consumed,
     no extra bow bonuses, same utility-axe exception.

4. Altar of Blessings
   - No class: "Choose <Class>" + "View Advancements" (unchanged).
   - Class chosen: "Choose" removed, "View Advancements" -> "Advance" (centred); other Base Class cards greyed + locked
     (switching = the existing class reset).
   - Advance / Choose Advancement with unmet requirements: a readable prompt ABOVE the Altar menu lists what is missing
     (level, the Advancement's Class skill Tier 7, Class Tier Points, pending tiers). Other Altar failures use the same
     prompt; unrelated vanilla messages and the requirements themselves are unchanged.

5. VFX (visual only)
   - Crescent Cleave: cracks kept + disturbed dirt, low dust and small grit following each half-buried wave (no spikes).
   - Impact Wave (normal + Ascended): dirt + dust dragged along with the crest. Frenzied Charge / Shield Charge: dust
     kicked up while dashing.
   - Halfmoon Slash: the slash scores the ground (crack arc + dirt along the cut).
   - Gravity Dominion + Horizon Walker's Gravity Blast: the glowing vortex is replaced by faint contracting air ripples,
     thin pale streaks drawn inward and loose dirt / dust crawling toward the centre (follows the moving orb, stops when
     it is high above the ground).
