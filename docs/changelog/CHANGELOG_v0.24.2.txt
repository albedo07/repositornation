IMMORTAL HEROES v0.24.2 - Ranger Polish
=======================================
Fixes
- Ranger Left Click chain and charged shots now fire arrows (the infinite-ammo patch never handed
  the arrow to the shot). Rangers can also draw / shoot with no arrows at all (basic arrow).
- Charged shot: releasing or cancelling too quickly no longer locks the charged shot for a while.
- Tumble Shot no longer triggers the vanilla dodge (which carried you back to your start). The body
  now does a real backflip; 0.5s i-frames (no damage, knockback or stagger).

Acrobat
- Gale Volley: swift 2m back-jump in 0.3s with 0.5s i-frames (same as Tumble Shot).
- Ascended Cyclone Arrow splits into 3 smaller cyclones the moment it first catches an enemy.
- Furious Winds: attack radius 10m, wind barrier 7m. Enemies can never get closer; inside the
  barrier they are pushed out gradually (Small 1m -> edge in 5s, Big / Boss in 8s). No enemy
  projectile gets inside the barrier. Ascended: 14m attack, 10m barrier.

Bowmaster
- Splitting Arrow (your design): stand still, 3 volleys of 5 arrows 0.5s apart into a 120 degree
  cone (Crescent Cleave), 15m; arrows pass through, every enemy in the cone is hit by each volley
  (90% per volley). Ascended: 5 volleys 0.25s apart, each hit stacks a Fire DoT (6s, refreshed).
- Starfall Volley: every 0.5s it hits EVERY enemy in the 20m area (45%); the falling arrows are
  cosmetic. Ascended final giant arrow hits every enemy in the area (300%).
- Ascended Ballista Shot (full charge): every enemy it pierces erupts in a 5m shockwave (60%).

New universal rule
- Ghost / passing-through skills trigger their after-effect on hit, never at the end of their
  range (applied to Cyclone Arrow and Ballista Shot; the other classes had none).

Art
- Ranger, Acrobat and Bowmaster skill icons (17), tree backgrounds (your Ranger scene left, the
  AC scene right, like every other class) and Altar cards (your portraits; Bowmaster emblem = a
  bow and arrow).

Settings reset to defaults once (Dev Mode OFF).
