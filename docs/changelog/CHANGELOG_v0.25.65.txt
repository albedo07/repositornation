Immortal Heroes v0.25.65 - Afterimages and Arrows

NO MORE BASIC ATTACKS OUT OF SKILLS (scan of every skill click)
- ROOT CAUSE: the click that finished a skill (e.g. Shield Charge's Hammer Slam) was also queued by Valheim as a
  normal attack and fired after the skill (the Staff fireball). Every skill that really starts now aborts the running
  attack and drops Valheim's queued attack input; the clicks that drive skills (Shield Charge slam, Halfmoon release,
  Arcane Phalanx volley) are swallowed; nothing queues while a skill holds you.
- Gun Staff stuck in its fire pose after Gravity Blast: skills used to only forget the running attack; it is now
  properly aborted (looping / hold attacks end cleanly).

JUMP SLAMS (Bonecrusher, Electric Smite) - back to the earlier direction, polished like Angel Comet
- Removed the vanilla axe-heavy attack (it played as a separate attack after landing) and the wide arm flare.
- In the air: arms only slightly out, then the weapon is cocked overhead while falling; ~0.17 s before the ground it
  swings into the smash (same timing trick as Angel Comet) - weapon driven into the ground, deep hero kneel.
- You still face your aim in the air; WASD only steers.

ATTACK SPEED (Masteries)
- Bonuses now scale each weapon's OWN speed (+50% = 1.5x its base), instead of squeezing every weapon onto one
  interval - a greatsword (Krom) stays heavier than a one-handed sword. Intervals inside the 1-2-1-2-3 chain stay
  even (the beat = the weapon's average swing). [Combat] ComboFixedInterval_v02565 = on brings back the fixed 0.6 s.

SWORD MASTER
- Moonlight Splitter / Halfmoon / Crescent Cleave: the crescents were nearly invisible (flat or edge-on to the camera,
  additive only). Now vivid opaque blue crescents with a white-hot core: horizontal ones tilt toward the camera,
  vertical ones (Crescent Cleave) are doubled so they read from behind and from the side. Less particle "fly" noise.
- Halfmoon Slash (Ultimate): a huge bright crescent sweeps out in front of you with every slash.
- Blade Storm: Vergil's Judgement Cut - a faded, pale sphere pops over the area (darker core, glowing rim), the
  cuts flash inside it, then it collapses.
- Eclipse (stronger): after the spin, an afterimage of the blade hails slashes through the whole radius (60% of the
  spin to everything inside). Ascended: the crescents then spiral upward around you and a second afterimage hails
  down (60%). Same radius as Eclipse. Tooltip updated.

RANGER
- Real arrows: every volley / rain / barrage arrow that lands leaves an arrow stuck in the ground (wooden shaft,
  steel head, fletching), arrows that hit a wall stay in it, and the big shots (sky arrow, Starfall finale, great
  arrows) leave a BIG glowing arrow planted like the Relics. They sink away after a few seconds.
- Furious Winds: a spinning dome of green wind around you (Neji's Rotation).
- Skyfall Barrage: aim range 20 m. Ascended: the finishing slam is a long diving leap onto the point you last aimed
  at (terrain only).
- Somersault Dance: always lands on terrain / buildings, never on a creature. Ascended: the arrow volley lands where
  you slammed (no more aiming it anywhere).
