AETHELBORN ASCENDED v0.25.193 - IN-GAME CHECKLIST
Not yet tested in game by Codex. Test both normal and Ascended where applicable.

1. Bow Master: hold Shift and move with an unloaded crossbow. Let reloading finish
   without releasing Shift. Fire, continue sprinting and allow the next reload to
   finish; repeat several times. No walking/stop/restarted progress while loading.
   Try both starting the reload during sprint and beginning sprint during reload.
   Verify ammo/stamina costs, reload speed and saved loaded state after re-equipping.
   Non-Bow-Master crossbows and equip/use actions must retain normal restrictions.
2. Acrobat: bow/greatbow drawing and firing still keep full current sprint speed;
   base Ranger and Bow Master bows/greatbows keep the intended vanilla slowdown.
3. Horizon Walker: normal/gun staff moving fire uses vanilla pose/animation,
   retains full current movement speed and keeps the existing projectile cadence.
4. Activate normal and Ascended Arcane Phantom, Phalanx and Rift Echo. Open
   inventory, chest/container, chat/text input, map, escape menu, Altar, skill tree
   and F8. Click/hold either mouse button and press skill bindings in each UI.
   No new Phantom/Echo shots or sword launches; an armed Phalanx volley stays armed.
   After closing, deliberately attack the world: all follow-ups work normally.
   Repeat after switching focus to another application. Existing world projectiles
   and already-released skill effects may finish; they are not cancelled globally.
5. Open UI while holding a staff charge, Meteor/Cataclysm, Ballista or Ascended
   Explosive Arrow. The held charge should cancel, with no new release shot.
   Check normal charging/release still works in world input. Paid costs stay paid.
6. Check other classes' skill hotkeys and channel follow-up clicks in chat/menus.
   Then confirm hotbar drag/rebind, skill-tree K and developer F8 remain usable.
7. Ranger, Acrobat, Bow Master: staves/wands/gun staves cannot be equipped.
   Sorcerer, Archmage, Horizon Walker: bows/greatbows/crossbows cannot be equipped.
   Check changing class with forbidden gear already equipped: it should be safely
   unequipped into inventory. No-class characters must remain unrestricted.
8. Fire effects listed in CHANGELOG: visibly burning ground in daylight/night,
   no tall smoke wall, preserved impact visuals. Crescent trail expires within
   its existing two-second segment lifetime; hit/tick/push behavior is unchanged.
9. Blade Storm normal/Ascended: soft sphere-like mist with clear slashes, the
   intended blue/dark-blue distinction, visible in daylight and against enemies.
   Check repeated casts plus overlapping fire for visual density and FPS.
10. Existing buff timers, cape, NACC, damage and other skills remain regression checks.
