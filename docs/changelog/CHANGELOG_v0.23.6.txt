IMMORTAL HEROES v0.23.6 - ASTRAL TWINS
======================================
(First start resets every setting to default once, Dev Mode OFF.)

HORIZON WALKER
- Ascended Afterimage Arsenal: the 3 mimics are now Astral twins - purple ghost copies of
  YOUR character (armour, shield, both Gun Staves...) that replay every movement and
  animation 0.2s late: run, strafe, roll, attack. They follow teleports and Rifts, and are
  rebuilt when you change equipment.
- They now fire while you hold Mouse1: every real shot / swing (each Gun Staff burst, each
  melee hit) is copied 0.2s later for 100% of your weapon damage.
- Gravity Blast: recast while the orb is out - 1st recast stops it where it is, 2nd recast
  makes it explode (130%).

ARCHMAGE
- Astral Greatblade: no charging any more (1s wind up, one slam).
- Ascended Astral Greatblade: you cannot move during the three slams.
- Ascended Astral Railcannon: 15% of the normal shot per tick (was 9%).
- Charged skills (Meteor Fall, Elemental Cataclysm): at max stacks you can keep holding as
  long as you like (no movement); a ring follows your aim and the skill lands where you
  aim when you release.
- Charge counter on the hotbar icon while charging (x/3 with the time to the next stack,
  no cooldown shade), plus the "Meteor Fall x/3" message again.

UI
- Highlights redone: a soft gold halo generated from the real border of each node frame,
  hotbar icon and +/- button (hover = soft, selected = strong).
- Locked Grace boxes (tree node + footer): the placeholder no longer covers the gold inner
  bevel unevenly, so the frame looks even on every side.
