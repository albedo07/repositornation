Aethelborn Ascended v0.25.176 - Hit Caps
Baseline: v0.25.175. Only new F8 options; defaults keep today's behaviour.

PERSISTENT DAMAGE HIT CAPS (user: option for how many times persistent damage can hit an enemy, every skill)
- Persistent damage = a hitbox that stays / travels and re-hits enemies inside it on an interval. DoTs / burns
  (Fire Burn, Spirit Burn, ...) are NOT persistent damage and are never counted.
- One cast's hits on the same enemy are counted; once the cap is reached that cast stops damaging that enemy (other
  effects of later ticks on it are skipped too). 0 = no limit.
- New key MaxPersistentHits_v025176 (default 0 = no limit, as now) in:
  Sword Master Moonlight Splitter Ascended (final wave) - Paladin Shield Charge - Paladin Electric Smite (all 16
  trails together) - Paladin Righteous Strike Ascended (all 12 trails together) - Paladin Judgement Hammer (throw +
  Ascended return together) - Priest Grand Cross - Spellcaster Gravity Blast (orb ticks; end burst not counted).
- Already had one (unchanged): Crescent Cleave MaxHitsPerWave_v025175 (2), Crescent Cleave Ascended FireTrailMaxHits (8).
- Shared helper DragonCombat.PersistentHit (per-cast counter that disappears with the cast).

BLADE STORM (user: option for how many slashes)
- Sword Master Judgement Cut: SpheresPerActivation_v025176 (2), CutsPerSphere_v025176 (3, normal + Ascended),
  Stacks_v025176 (4 stored activations, normal).
- Sword Master Blade Storm Ascended: SpheresPerActivation_v025176 (4); its Stacks key is unchanged.
- Each cut keeps the damage it has at the default layout, so more spheres / cuts = more total damage.
