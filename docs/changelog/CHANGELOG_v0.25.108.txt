Immortal Heroes v0.25.108 - Live Studio
Base: v0.25.107. Only the F9 Animation Studio and its preview copy (combat animations unchanged).

FIXES (why the dots did not move things)
- The first and last frame of every animation had 0 blend weight, so weapon aim, two-hand grip and
  legs did nothing there. While posing every frame is now shown at full weight.
- Legs on skills built on Valheim's own swing were frozen (NoPlant); the Studio copy always plants now.
- Knee dot = WHERE the knee goes: the foot is solved under it, the whole leg bends with it (hip limit:
  knee up to a bit above hip height). Ankle dot = where the foot goes (never below the ground).
- Weapon dots: the held item is now found on the copy even without an attach point, so the WHITE dot
  sits on the real weapon tip and the weapon turns with it.
