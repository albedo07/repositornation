Immortal Heroes v0.25.36 - Hammer Slam
======================================
- Shield Charge finisher is now a HAMMER SLAM with your main-hand weapon (was a shield bash):
  the charge stops, the hammer goes up and comes down hard (Valheim's club heavy attack), damage on the slam (0.4 s).
- Left Click during the charge now reliably triggers the slam (the click was often missed before).
- The charge still ends in the slam on its own at full distance / after 4 hits on a Big or Boss.
- No leftover shield-up pose over the slam. Tooltip lines say "Hammer Slam".
