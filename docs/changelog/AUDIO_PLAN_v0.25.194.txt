AETHELBORN ASCENDED - NEXT AUDIO PASS (PLAN ONLY)
2026-10-17

Use Valheim's existing sound prefabs and ZSFX playback. No audio changes are
included in v194. The actual supplied game assembly contains ZSFX.Play,
SetVolumeModifier, SetPitchModifier, Stop, FadeOut, m_audioClips and built-in
concurrency settings; ZNetScene exposes GetPrefab and GetPrefabNames.

1. Build a one-time catalog of the installed game's sfx_ prefabs. Preview them
   in F8 and choose verified names. Do not guess prefab IDs or extract/reship
   the game's sound files. Cache chosen prefab/clip references after lookup.
2. Map a small number of events per skill: preparation (when needed), release,
   impact and an optional short tail/loop for a persistent field. Position sounds
   at the casting weapon, moving projectile/field or actual impact as appropriate.
3. Reuse weapon swishes and cuts for Sword Master, heavy impacts/ground rumble
   for Mercenary, lightning/healing sounds for Cleric's line, native elemental
   sounds for Sorcerer and bow/projectile sounds for Ranger. Preview the exact
   choices in game rather than committing to names not yet verified.
4. Keep existing 3D attenuation/mixer behavior and ZSFX concurrency limits. Use
   modest per-skill volume and slight native pitch variation. Persistent damage
   ticks should not create a new full-volume sound every hit; throttle/loop them.
5. Provide F8 master/per-skill volume and sound enable controls. Keep normal and
   Ascended mappings independently tunable, with a restrained extra layer for
   the upgrade. Match sound events to actual attack contact/release, not GUI clicks.
6. Stop skill-owned loops on dodge cancellation/end/death. For multiplayer, send
   the sound event once to nearby listeners; avoid playing both a local copy and
   a network copy of the same event. Verify this with two clients before calling
   the sound pass finished.

This reuses the game's audio assets and native playback instead of requiring
new audio files. Exact sound choices and final mixing still need an in-game pass.
