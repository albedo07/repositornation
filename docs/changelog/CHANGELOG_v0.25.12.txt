IMMORTAL HEROES v0.25.12 - ANGEL COMET AND BURNS

PALADIN
- Angel Comet impact: 95 Blunt -> 300 Blunt + 80 Fire + 140 Spirit (520). A 0-star Troll (600 HP) is left at
  about 1/8 HP at Tier 0; higher Tiers can one-shot it.
- Electric Smite landing (stays just above Angel Comet): 220 Blunt + 100 Fire + 240 Lightning + 120 Spirit (680).
- Burns: Ascended Angel Comet ring 6 -> 20 Fire + 20 Spirit per second; Electric Smite 6 -> 18 Fire, 9 -> 24 Spirit.

UNIVERSAL STACKING BURNS (every skill that applies Fire Burn or Spirit Burn, every class)
- Each consecutive Burn tick on the same target hits harder than the previous one: +20% of the base tick per
  tick, up to x4 ([Damage Over Time] StackingBurnPercentPerTick / StackingBurnMaxMultiplier).
- Several burns ticking together share one step; the chain resets once the target stops burning (1.6s gap).
