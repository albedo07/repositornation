ROOT CAUSE FOUND: SILENCE AT THE START OF RECORDINGS
Several recordings begin with dead silence, so a sound played "on the cast" was only heard later and landed on the
wrong moment (MagicSword2 0.57 s, Laserbeam 0.63 s, Slam 0.63 s, DBZ 0.48 s, IceSlam 0.40 s, BlackHole2 0.39 s,
Teleport 0.37 s, BuffClock 0.28 s, MagicSword 0.12 s). The silence is trimmed off (a 5 ms pre-roll and a 3 ms fade-in
are kept; nothing else changed) and every hit point was re-measured.
