IMMORTAL HEROES - USER ANIMATION GUIDE (2026-10-06, overrides every earlier animation decision)
Storyboards: docs/reference/animation_blueprint_v02514/storyboards (follow them meticulously).

DEFAULT WEAPON BEHAVIOURS
1. Wherever the hand points, the equipped item points (raised hand = weapon up; hand east = tip east). Universal.
2. The off-hand weapon / shield stays stationary or behaves normally unless stated.
3. No extra movement that breaks immersion (no flailing left arm, no legs flailing in the air).
