All 20 animation families
  • ANIM_01 · Olympic Hero — A near-horizontal aerial barrel roll into a main-hand superhero landing. The same performance reads holy or brutal through VFX and weight, not different body choreography.
  • ANIM_02 · Sky Command — One shared call-and-lower motion for distant sky summons and overhead bombardment.
  • ANIM_03 · Wavecaller — A short gather-and-open gesture for radial healing, buffs, barriers and self-centered bursts.
  • ANIM_04 · War Cry — A compact chest-led rally with a larger deliberate version and a small automatic reaction.
  • ANIM_05 · Arc Cutter — A reusable coil–cut–follow sequence whose plane and sequence count match the skill.
  • ANIM_06 · Earthbreaker — A shared grounded power-stroke family with separately authored descending and rising paths.
  • ANIM_07 · Ground Seal — A reusable low weight shift for a grounded command, stomp, or trap placement.
  • ANIM_08 · Flash Draw — A short sword command that causes several cuts at a remote point.
  • ANIM_09 · Cyclone — A grounded yaw rotation with interchangeable entry, loop and exit.
  • ANIM_10 · Vanguard — A low forward drive sharing leg/torso motion while hand profiles distinguish shield bash from sword thrust.
  • ANIM_11 · Comet Dive — A streamlined head-leading travel pose with a safe exit.
  • ANIM_12 · Power Pitch — One torso-coil and release family for thrown or batted magical projectiles.
  • ANIM_13 · Pulse Drive — A compact chamber-and-push that reads as punch, cone blast, targeted blessing or orb launch.
  • ANIM_14 · Rift Conductor — Short mobile command gestures for effects that operate independently of the caster.
  • ANIM_15 · Ghost Step — A teleport transition that preserves the motion already underway.
  • ANIM_16 · Siege Caster — A braced aim/charge hold with a controlled magical release.
  • ANIM_17 · Deadeye Draw — A common bow/crossbow aim–release family. This animation name is separate from Bowmaster’s Deadeye mastery.
  • ANIM_18 · Rebound Shot — A true backwards somersault while retreating, with a forward-facing aerial release.
  • ANIM_19 · Sky Archer — A shared raised-bow release and bombardment support family with a separate airborne hover module.
  • ANIM_20 · Phoenix Rise — A restrained survival-proc recovery accent that reads as being restored without acting like a knockdown.

Olympic Hero is the horizontal aerial barrel roll into a main-hand superhero landing. Sky summons share Sky Command; outward buffs share Wavecaller. Shared motion does not imply shared gameplay mechanics.
