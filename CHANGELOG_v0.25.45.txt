Immortal Heroes v0.25.45 - Class Chains

- The 5-hit chain (1 > 2 > 1 > 2 > 3 + 1 s lockout) now only works when your class is fit for the weapon:
  Sword Master = Swords; Warrior / Mercenary = any melee weapon; Cleric / Paladin / Priest = Maces / Clubs;
  Kali (later) = Knives / Fist weapons. Everyone else (no class, Sorcerer, Ranger, wrong weapon) keeps
  Valheim's normal chain.
