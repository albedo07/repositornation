Aethelborn Ascended v0.25.255 - Whirlwind Polish   (base: ANCHOR v0.25.254, user-confirmed)

WHIRLWIND CA (Normal and Ascended)
- Dual wield (two one-handed weapons): both weapons now stay visible. Both arms reach forward side by side and each
  weapon points straight ahead from its own hand (the two-hand grip that hid the off-hand weapon is no longer used here).
  Shields and torches are still put away during the spin as before.
- Spears and atgeirs: these are held near the middle, so the farthest point could be the butt. The head is now found as
  the WIDER end of the model (spearhead / blade vs bare shaft) and that end points ahead. Log line:
  "pole weapon ends ... - head ahead / behind, flipped".
- Two-handed swords: the left hand now goes on the handle BEHIND the right fist (toward the pommel) instead of in front of
  it, and turns only halfway to the right hand's angle - a natural two-hand sword grip. Axes/maces/other weapons unchanged.
- Pause (Esc): while the game is paused the last drawn arm pose is held, so the hands no longer wind/spin around the
  blade (Therzie weapons).
- No damage, timing, VFX or sound change.
