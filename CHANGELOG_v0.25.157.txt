v0.25.157 Paired Guard (2HDW animation ownership only; heavy attacks, damage, Mercenary restrictions, SBDW 1-2-1-2-3 timing untouched)
- Loot panel: NOT part of Aethelborn Ascended (no loot/pickup UI in any of our 10 DLLs; nothing disabled). See TEST_RESULTS for how to find the owner.
- 2HDW controller (scimitars, Hellblade cleavers, paired knives) is now validated before EVERY accepted primary swing and again right before the swing trigger fires: it must be the animator's current controller, must really replace Attack1-3 of the base controller (mask 7), and must expose the swing_axe/axe_swing x3 carrier. A mod that swaps the controller in between (Therzie Warfare's AnimationReplaceManager) is detected, ours is re-asserted once; if it still fails the cause is written to LogOutput.log ("WARNING [2HDW] ...") and shown once on screen - no silent fallback.
- Skill takeover releases the 2HDW override (only if it is ours); the next accepted primary swing re-applies and re-validates it. Before this, the override stayed up to 1 s after the last swing, so a skill's vanilla swing_axe/axe_secondary/knife/mace triggers played SBDW's replaced clips.
