2. Cape clipping (Bonecrusher and every jump slam / Mixamo / clip-driven skill animation):
   - Cause: Valheim capes are MagicaCloth2; their inertia constraint keeps the cloth in world space while our skill
     animations move the body far and fast (leap, fall, hips pinned, travel), so the cape lagged, flipped forward and
     folded through the torso.
   - Fix (DragonClothGuard): while a skill animation drives the local player, the cape's inertia is anchored to the
     player and world/local inertia damped to 10%; blended back over 0.4 s afterwards. Cape never hidden; colliders,
     gravity, wind, CA, movement and Rigidbody untouched. Log line "[Aethelborn] Cape guard: MagicaCloth2 inertia ..."
     shows which fields were found.
Everything else (Crescent push, AA NACC, skills, configs, HUD, class rules) unchanged.
