v0.25.154 NACC Handoff (animation handoffs only; no config keys / defaults / cooldowns / input delays changed)
- Ownership tokens: NaccGen (bumped when a skill or a takeoff takes over the chain) and SkillAnimGen (bumped when a normal attack is accepted). State learning, swing timing and the chain trim captured under an older NaccGen are dead; skill clip drivers, vanilla tails and loops started under an older SkillAnimGen retire themselves.
- Accepted skill takeover (SwallowAttackInput / BlockSkillAnimAttack / PlayClip -> DragonCombat.SkillTakeover): interrupted NACC counters, timing, chain speed and pending state learning are reset BEFORE the attack is aborted. A finisher that completed keeps its lockout; one the skill cut short leaves none. Truncated swings are never measured into the chain timing table.
- State learning only caches a validated swing: same controller as when learned, not looping, player really in a melee attack, no skill/Mixamo/vanilla-skill animation owning the animator, clip not locomotion / skill-named. Base-class captured states are also dropped on a controller change.
- Accepted melee normal attack (Attack.Start postfix) retires the superseded skill animation: clip driver, Mixamo graph, vanilla tail/loop, forced run, skill speed override. Damage / VFX coroutines the skill already started are untouched.
- No airborne NACC: Humanoid.StartAttack is rejected (no fall-through to vanilla) for the chained PRIMARY melee attack of a fitting weapon while airborne (no ground contact, or none for > 0.06 s; swimming/attached/riding excluded), before any combo state or trigger. Takeoff resets the chain and drops the queued primary click, so landing starts at strike one. Finisher lockouts kept. Heavy (secondary) attacks, non-NACC weapons and jump skills (Circle Swing, RJS...) are not touched.
- Results: TEST_RESULTS_v0.25.154.txt (logic model tools/tests/nacc_handoff, not Valheim).
