3. [Structures] Batched Building Drawing = false (EXPERIMENT - turn on to test).
   Repeated building meshes in 64 m cells are drawn with GPU instancing instead of one draw call per piece; the original
   renderers are only hidden. Colliders, support, damage, saves and other players are untouched (client-only, picture-only).
   Safety (all checked in assembly_valheim IL):
   - only materials Valheim already marks instancing-capable;
   - any per-piece material change goes through MaterialMan (hover highlight, roof snow, Ashlands tint) - that piece
     returns to normal drawing for good, so its look stays correct;
   - health look changes (new/worn/broken) rebuild the piece; pieces with LOD groups, animators, doors, signs, fires,
     item/armor stands, moving bodies are never batched;
   - batched renderers are re-checked in the background; any error = everything back to normal drawing, logged once.
   Report: BatchedPieces, BatchedExcludedPieces, BatchGroups, BatchedDrawCallsLastFrame, BatchedInstancesLastFrame.
   Uses the Building Pieces draw distance. Off by default until a capture shows it helps on your PC.
