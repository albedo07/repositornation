CORRECTION of my v0.4.2 analysis: I said distant objects load 100 per tick. After reading Valheim's CreateObjects /
CreateDistantObjects from your assembly_valheim.dll: near + distant share ONE counter capped at 10 per tick
(100 only in the loading screen). So "spread out loading" would change nothing and was NOT built. The 46-62-piece load
frames must come from somewhere else - most likely world-zone generation (terrain, vegetation and location buildings
spawned together by ZoneSystem.SpawnZone). This build measures that instead of guessing.

1. Report line "SlowFramesByCause": every frame >= 33 ms gets one cause - Unload / ZoneGeneration / PieceLoad /
   MemoryCleanup / Other - with count and worst ms. RunZoneSpawns counts zones generated.
2. [Memory] Longer Cleanup Slices = false (EXPERIMENT, opt-in), Cleanup Slice Milliseconds = 5 (Unity default 3).
   Your game has incremental GC on with 3 ms slices, yet 20-46 ms cleanup frames appear every 7-10 s: garbage outpaces
   the slices and Unity finishes in one go. ON gives each slice more time. Owned value, restored when off; only applies
   when incremental GC is active. Whether it helps is exactly what the next capture pair answers.
Unchanged: unloading spread, draw distance, low shadows, everything else.
