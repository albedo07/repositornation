Aethelborn Ascended v0.25.268 - Soft Endings   (base: v0.25.267; ANCHOR v0.25.257)

RULE (user): no sound effect or music is ever cut - it always fades out gradually.

MAIN MENU MUSIC
- Leaving the main menu (or turning [Main Menu] Use AA Main Menu off) fades the music out over 2.5 s instead of stopping it.
  Valheim's own music comes back once the fade has finished. Coming back to the menu restores full volume.

PALADIN / PRIEST
- Goddess Relic, Lightning Relic, Holy Relic (Priest), Judgement Hammer - Normal and Ascended: the swing sound is removed.
  Their other sounds are unchanged (landing sounds, hammer spin). The swing sliders are hidden in F8.
- Electric Smite - Normal and Ascended: each of the 16 lightning trails now carries the Electric sound. It travels with
  the trail head (louder near you, softer far away) and fades out when the trail ends. Each trail starts at a different
  point of the recording so they don't sound like one copy, and they share the level so 16 together are not deafening.
  New slider: Paladin Electric Smite Audio > Lightning Trails Volume (%). Ascended storm sounds unchanged.
- Angel Comet - Normal and Ascended: the BigWhoosh2 whoosh now plays during the whole aerial wind-up, from the cast,
  following you, looping if the wind-up is longer than the recording, and fading out at the slam. Before, it was timed
  so that its loudest point hit at the slam. Landing sounds (HeavyPunch + BassDrop) and the Ascended aftershock
  (SolidBoom) are unchanged.

CHECKS: all ten modules compile (C# 5) with the installer's reference set. Not tested in game.
