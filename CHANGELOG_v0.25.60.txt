Immortal Heroes v0.25.60 - Ground Lightning

LIGHTNING TRAILS (Electric Smite's 16 trails, Ascended Righteous Strike's 12 trails)
- Real lightning now crawls along the ground with each trail: a jagged, flickering bolt (soft glow + white-hot
  core) that follows the terrain from the impact to the trail's head, re-forking every 0.05 s, never sinking into
  the ground. The tail dims, the head crackles with sparks and carries a small light, a fork snaps off the bolt
  now and then, and when the trail ends the bolt fades and leaves a scorched scar on the ground.
- Replaces the old little rings drawn every frame (they stay as the fallback with [Visuals] EnhancedSkillVfx off).
- Visual only: damage, range and timing unchanged.
