Aethelborn Ascended v0.25.260 - Greatsword Skills   (base: ANCHOR v0.25.257 + v0.25.258/259)

TWO-HANDED SWORD (and scythe) SKILL CAs - slow / out of sync
- Cause: Valheim gives greatswords (and scythes) their own animator weapon state. Most skill CAs borrow vanilla
  battleaxe / mace / sword / atgeir swings; under the greatsword state those swings do not play the way they do with
  their own weapon, so the CA ran slow and drifted from the hit timing (the same problem dual wield had, v0.25.161).
- Fix (all skills, Normal and Ascended): while a skill CA that borrows a vanilla swing plays with a greatsword/scythe
  equipped, the animator is held in the state that swing belongs to (battleaxe -> two-handed axe, atgeir -> atgeir,
  mace/sword/axe/knife -> one-handed). Valheim's own weapon setup puts the real greatsword state back as soon as the
  skill and its play-out are over. Greatsword swings themselves are left alone.
- Affected when wielding a two-handed sword: every skill that fires vanilla swings, e.g. Heavy Slash / Ascended Heavy
  Slash strikes, Circle Swing chain, Halfmoon Slash, Mercenary/Sword Master swing-based skills. Other weapons: unchanged.
- One log line per weapon state + swing: "Skill CA with a Greatsword weapon: animator held in ... state for '...'".
- No damage, timing, VFX or sound changes.

CHECKS: all ten modules compile (C# 5) against your real DLLs. Not tested in game.
