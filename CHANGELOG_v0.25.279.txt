MAIN MENU LOGO - EXACTLY WHERE VALHEIM'S WAS

  downscaling, no edge fringes). Unity then draws it with Valheim's own position, scale and animation, so it lands where
  the original title was. No position maths of ours is involved any more.
- Nothing is painted over Valheim's logo; Valheim's files are untouched. The embers keep drifting around it.
- F8 [Main Menu] Logo Scale / Offset X / Offset Y still work (the picture is rebuilt once per change).
