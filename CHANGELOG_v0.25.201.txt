Aethelborn Ascended v0.25.201 - Embedded Gems
Baseline: v0.25.200 Wind Rings and Bird Targets (ChatGPT passes 192-200, imported unchanged).

1. RELICS (Paladin Goddess Relic, Priest Holy Relic)
- Cause of "floating / disappearing by angle": v0.25.200 gems were thin plates seated INSIDE or
  within 1% of the relic surfaces (centre gem inside the seal disc, side gems on the panel face,
  heart 0.025 thickness from the disc and sunburst) -> z-fighting.
- Gems are now SET stones: a gold bezel (collar) passes through the relic and the stone passes
  through the bezel, both rising clearly (>= 6% of thickness) above front and back faces
  (`IhSetGem`). One piece with the relic, readable from every angle, no coplanar faces.
- Holy Relic heart: gold heart frame through the whole relic body + green heart through the frame
  + blue gem through the heart (no bezel). No surface is shared with the disc or sunburst.

2. JUDGEMENT HAMMER
- Head = symmetrical horizontal block: square cross-section, length 0.7 of v0.25.200, no 2.4x
  vertical stretch (`HammerHeadPoint`). Head bottom stays on the collar.
- Growth is UNIFORM (`UpdateHammerVisual` scales all axes by height / 1.21). Gameplay hitbox
  and growth rate unchanged.

3. PERMANENT BADGES (skill tree, all Advancement Classes)
- The painted padlock badges on the AC permanent slots (Signature 1, Signature 2, Ultimate) were
  cut by the skill art. The badge texture is now stamped ON TOP of frame + art at the painted
  position (25 px, Ultimate 29 px), same as the Base Class badge (`IhDrawFrameOverlay`).
