IMMORTAL HEROES v0.20.8 - CLERIC PER FRAMEWORK

MECHANICS (now match the Framework doc)
- Cleric's Blessing: +35 Max HP, +20% HP Regen, and no movement penalty from Shields,
  Staves and one-handed Club weapons (two-handed Clubs excluded). Paladin and Priest keep it.
  (1.5x shield Block Force / Armor and Staff + Shield were already in.)
- Holy Trinity (Paladin Mastery) replaces the old Elemental Savant / Holy Knight choice.
  With a Club-type melee weapon + any Shield: +15 Clubs (effective cap 100), no Armor
  movement penalty, Slash and Pierce each raised to at least 50% of the hit's Blunt
  (never lowered).
- Heaven's Crucible (Priest Grace): you and allies within 10m at cast get their own 250 HP
  Barrier; Barrier Armor = 30% of your Armor, snapshotted at cast; 16s or until broken;
  10 min cooldown from activation; no cost. The old permanent +30% Priest Armor passive
  is retired (it is not in the Framework).
- Bless Thy Sinners (Priest Mastery): recovery 50% HP over 6s (was 7s), +50% Move Speed and
  -70% Stamina Usage for all actions during those 6s (was +100% Stamina Regen); ally save
  cooldown 40 min per ally (was 35). Self 20 min and the 20m radius were already right.
  New values use new .cfg keys, so they apply even if your .cfg had the old ones.

COLOURS
- Non-damaging skills are Green: Holy Wave is now Green (frame, hotbar icon, tooltip values).
- Divine Intervention deals damage, so it is Cyan again (header SUPPORT).

UI
- Heaven's Crucible tooltip uses the same lines as Heaven's Light (Cost None, minutes).
- F8 hides the retired Paladin passive sections.
