Immortal Heroes v0.25.69 - Sharp Edges

HUD
- The painted stat HUD is smaller by default: 430 px wide at 1080p (key FrameWidth_v02569).
- The old dark backdrop behind the HUD is gone when the painted frame is shown.
- Stamina is yellow and Eitr is purple again, like the old bars.
- Both HUDs (stat HUD and skill hotbar) can now be resized freely. Open the inventory, hover a HUD, then:
  - mouse wheel = bigger / smaller
  - Shift + wheel = wider / narrower
  - Ctrl + wheel = taller / shorter
  - middle click = reset
  - The values are saved in [Immortal HUD]: StatHudWidthScale, StatHudHeightScale, SkillBarWidthScale and SkillBarHeightScale.

VFX / animation
- Getsuga Tenshou crescents (Moonlight Splitter, Crescent Cleave, Halfmoon Slash):
  - The cutting edge now leads in the travel direction, with the tips swept back.
  - Sleeker and sharper: a thin razor body, a tight halo and a white cutting edge.
  - Horizontal slashes lie in the cut plane, tilted toward the camera.
  - Vertical slashes stand upright, as two blades 14 degrees apart, so they read from behind.
- Knight's Guidance (Grace): toned down. A dim, thin column and a plain ring, with no ground glow.
- Circle Swing crow hop: the off hand braces in front of the chest. This covers a shield or the Mercenary's off-hand weapon.
- Stomp: chicken-wing flare. The elbows are bent and pushed out, with the hands at the hips, instead of arms stretched wide.
