IMMORTAL HEROES v0.23.8 - Icons
===============================
Fixes
- Cleric's Blessing +35 HP now really raises max HP (added where Valheim stores max health;
  before only the bar showed 170 while health was capped at the food value).
- Hyper Armor = no knockback (push force zeroed on every hit while Hyper Armor is active).

Priest
- Holy Parry: the "next skill empowered" buff is announced once and kept until a skill uses it;
  after use, parries can't grant it again for 25s ([Priest Holy Shockwave] EmpowerCooldown).
  Hyper Armor on every parry and the 15s Holy Shockwave are unchanged.
- Lightning Relic / Holy Relic radius 7m (was 10m). Consecrated Ground radius 10m (was 15m).
- Grand Cross width 7m (was 15m).
- Ascended Grand Cross: width 12m (was 20m), no end burst; every enemy hit is Exposed (6s)
  and takes double Spirit Burn.

Art
- Skill icons for Sword Master, Mercenary, Archmage and Horizon Walker (all 34 skills + Graces),
  same style as the Cleric icons (tools/paint_kit_icons.py). Placeholder initials are gone.
  Tree, hotbar, HUD and Altar all use them.

Framework doc updated. Settings reset to defaults once (Dev Mode OFF).
