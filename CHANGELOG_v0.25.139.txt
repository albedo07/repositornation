Aethelborn Ascended v0.25.139 - Face Aim   (baseline: user anchor v0.25.138v)

1. FACE THE AIM
- Every skill with a wind up >= 1 s now turns to the crosshair until its hit (Sky Summons included).
- Ascended Astral Greatblade follows the aim through all 3 slams (they used to go where the camera pointed, the body stayed behind).
- Rift Echo follows the aim for its whole duration; Phalanx Volley faces the aim on release.

2. PAIRED 2H WEAPONS (Warfare Scimitar Swords, Hellblade Cleavers...)
- Counted as paired when 2H + prefab/anim contains "dual" / "scimitar" / "cleaver" or its animation is registered by Warfare / Wizardry.
- They use the DUAL WIELD NACC rules (dual timing keys) and our chain always applies (chain levels forced to 3 if the weapon has fewer). Heavy attack untouched.
- Log line "[NACC] paired weapon: ..." prints once per weapon (prefab, skill, animations, chain levels). If a weapon still misbehaves send me that line.

3. RUN JUMP SLAM JITTER (root cause)
- The ground-reach spine bend was re-applied on top of itself every rendered frame (the Animator does not rewrite the spine every frame) and the weapon probe measured the already-bent pose = violent shaking while looking for terrain. The spine is now restored to the animator pose before each bend (same guard idea as the clip driver).

4. PLAYERS ARE GHOSTS TO SKILLS
- Ranger + Sorcerer skill projectiles / aim casts ignore EVERY player's colliders, not only yours.
- Fat arrows (Splitting Arrow's great arrow, Ballista...): the sphere always overlapped the ground at your feet and "hit" it at distance 0, planting a giant arrow inside you. Solids are now tested by the arrow's centre line only.

5. OUR MOD ON TOP
- All our Harmony patches (CombatRuntime, Advanced, Sorcerer, Ranger) now run FIRST (prefixes) / LAST (postfixes) unless a priority was set explicitly.
- Warfare / Wizardry animation-table lookup cached (was a scan of every loaded assembly per call).
