Immortal Heroes v0.19.2 - Test focus (Dragon's Altar)

1. Interact with the Dragon's Altar: the new window opens centered (Esc / X closes).
2. Click Warrior / Cleric / Sorcerer: gold frame moves, right panel updates (title, text, kit, emblems).
3. Choose Cleric -> confirmation card -> Yes, Confirm: status shows Base class: Cleric.
4. View Advancements (or click the Paladin / Priest emblem): Advancement page, gold ring on the
   selected emblem, SKILLS + PASSIVE at the bottom left, < Base Classes goes back.
5. Choose Paladin with the requirements unmet: the Altar refuses and lists what is missing.
6. Reset (top left): press twice within 5 s to reset the class.
7. Check text fits at your resolution (1080p / 1440p) and nothing overlaps.
