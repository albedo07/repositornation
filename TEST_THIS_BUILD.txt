Immortal Heroes v0.20.9 - Test focus (Priest Buckler Parry)

1. Priest + Bronze or Iron Buckler: parry an attack (block right as it lands).
   -> "Holy Shockwave - next skill empowered", gold ring 10m, enemies around take Spirit
   damage and stagger (a boss does not stagger).
2. Parry again within 15s -> "Holy Parry - next skill empowered" (no shockwave).
3. After a parry, cast Grand Cross: its hits deal ~1.75x. Cast it again: normal.
   Cast Holy Wave first: the empowerment stays for the next damaging skill.
4. With a round shield (not a buckler) or as Paladin: nothing happens on parry.
5. Hyper Armor: after a parry you are not staggered for 5s.
