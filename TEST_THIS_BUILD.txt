Immortal Heroes v0.15.0 - Test focus

1. Hotbar area should match the approved preview (docs/previews/PREVIEW_v0.15.0_*.png).
2. Click a skill: gold ring around the icon, + centered right under its name.
   Press +: - and + side by side, CONFIRM plaque appears over the compass. Click CONFIRM.
3. Close the tree with pending points: they are discarded.
4. Click "3" under slot 3, press F: label shows F. Click "4", press F: 3 and 4 swap.
   Right-click a label: back to default. Esc while "PRESS KEY": cancels (tree stays open).
5. Click "M4 + R": press a modifier, then a key. Right-click resets to M4 + R.
6. Restart the game: bindings persist (BepInEx/config, [Hotbar] section).
