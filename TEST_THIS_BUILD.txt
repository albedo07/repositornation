Normal versions (Paladin character):
1. Goddess Relic: smaller Troll-sized cross, 5m impact.
2. Ray of Hope + Holy Wave: instant, ~0.5s stop, main hand raised.
3. Judgement Hammer (old Divine Verdict key): hammer flies 20m along your aim, grows as it travels,
   passes through enemies, stops at walls, Cripples.

(any subset works). Restart or reload the config, then test each skill. Tree tooltips read "ASCENDED - ...".
