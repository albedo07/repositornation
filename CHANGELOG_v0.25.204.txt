Aethelborn Ascended v0.25.204 - Clean Borders (visuals only; relics untouched as approved)

1. BLADE STORM: the ball of light inside is about 1.5x bigger (halo, white heart and mist). The 5 curved slashes are
   unchanged.
2. GROUND CRACKS DOUBLED: Seismic Guillotine, Impact Wave and Astral Greatblade drew every crack twice - once as the
   full-length scar and again under each dirt furrow, with slightly different jitter - so the overlap looked brighter.
   The furrows now add only the dirt (`Furrow(..., scar false)`), so each crack is drawn once. Crescent Cleave keeps
   its furrow scar, since that is its only crack.
3. ASCENDED HEAVY SLASH 3RD STRIKE: the overhead chop's slash was drawn in a perfectly vertical plane running straight
   away from the camera, so from behind the player it was edge-on and invisible. It is now tilted 25 deg off vertical
   (still reads as the overhead chop) and slightly wider.
4. SKILL TREE BADGES (every class): the painted lock emblems are removed from the Signature and Ultimate frames. Each
   corner is rebuilt as plain border from the frame's mirrored, intact corner, blended into the art around it - on
   the tree canvas and on the colour overlays (Ascended red / magenta, ...). Every permanent skill (Ascended,
   Signature, Ultimate) now gets the same single stamped badge, at the Base Class position and size.
   Preview: docs/previews/PREVIEW_v0.25.204_Clean_Badges.png (top = rebuilt borders, bottom = with the stamp).
