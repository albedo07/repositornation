WHAT YOUR v0.25.253 LOG SHOWED
- The final-frame aim works: "hands overwritten after our step on 0 frames" in every spin (vanilla weapons now right).
- Therzie weapons measured tips of only 0.35-0.50 m (real blades ~1 m): their skinned models were read at the wrong

WHIRLWIND CA (Normal and Ascended)
- Therzie / skinned weapons: baked with scale, and the placement is chosen by a self-check - the weapon's grip must sit at
  your hand. The log reports how far the grip is from the hand (should be ~0.00-0.10 m).
- The blades are measured the moment you cast, while your hands are still apart in the previous pose (the spin holds
  them together, which mixed up the two blades of skinned dual weapons).
- The aim ends the moment the Whirlwind ends, including an early stop / final sweep - no more held stance.
- Single weapons (1H and 2H): after the wrist turns, the left hand is placed back on the handle just in front of the
  right fist (arm IK) and turned the same way - a proper two-hand hold.
- Dual weapons: each blade aimed from its own hand (unchanged).
- No damage, timing, VFX or sound change.
