Aethelborn Ascended v0.25.280 - Menu Music Fade   (base: v0.25.279; ANCHOR v0.25.257)

MAIN MENU MUSIC - NEVER STOPS INSTANTLY
- Checked first: the menu music code was byte-for-byte unchanged since v0.25.271; the logo passes did not edit it.
- Two weak points found and fixed:
  1. The music ran inside the same error guard as the logo code, so a failing logo step could skip the music update.
     The music now has its own guard and always runs.
  2. The music only followed Valheim's own fade. If Valheim drops or cuts its menu track at once when the world starts
     loading, ours stopped on that same frame - the instant stop you heard. Now, the moment the world starts loading,
     our music runs its OWN 3-second fade (real time; a loading hitch counts at most 0.25 s) and stops only once silent.
- Everything else unchanged: it still replaces Valheim's menu track, starts with it, and the vanilla menu music never
  plays. Log: "[AA Menu] world loading: menu music fading out over 3 s (...)" then "... faded out (3 s)".

All v0.25.279 changes included (logo in place).
CHECKS: all ten modules compile (C# 5) with the installer's reference set. Not tested in game.
