Immortal Heroes v0.25.80 - One at a Time

SKILLS (every Class)
- No more multi-casting. Only one skill can run at a time, and it must finish before another can start: its wind up, any hold (charges, jump slams, channels) and its swing.
  - Pressing another skill too early does nothing and says "<skill> is still going" (shown at most once a second).
  - Pressing the SAME skill again still works: recasts, charge releases, follow-up shots and channel cancels.
  - There is always at least 0.45 s between two different skills.

HUD
- Warrior now shows its numbers inside the bars like every other Class: HP, Stamina | Eitr, and the level on EXP.
  - The old value column is gone, and the Warrior bars now run all the way to the end caps.

VFX
- Horizon Walker Rift Walker portals are now a standing oval of swirling void with a blazing violet rim and sparks, instead of a thin ring line.
