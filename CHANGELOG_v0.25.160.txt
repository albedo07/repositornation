v0.25.160 Trigger Path (2HDW primary swings only; heavy attacks, damage, class restrictions, skills, SBDW untouched)
- CAUSE (from our code + Harmony ordering): the 2HDW swing trigger is the weapon's native "dual_*<n>" name, which Therzie's Warfare AnimationReplaceManager keys its controller swap on. Our ZSyncAnimation.SetTrigger prefix was Priority.First like theirs; equal priorities run in registration order and Therzie's GUIDs ("Therzie.*", upper case) sort before ours, so their prefix ran FIRST: it swapped the animator to Warfare's controller and renamed the trigger, our prefix then failed its "starts with <base>" test and returned untouched - the native Therzie swing played, and none of the v0.25.157-159 checks (which sit after that test) ever ran. SBDW was never affected because one-handed swing triggers are vanilla names Warfare does not map.
- FIX (owns the whole trigger path instead of detecting): ComboTriggerPrefix priority 100000 (runs before every Priority.First prefix); it also recognises a trigger an earlier prefix already renamed (reverse lookup in Warfare's table) and still routes it. The routed carrier + our paired controller are carried through ZSyncAnimation.RPC_SetTrigger (where the trigger reaches the animator): prefix at Priority.Last puts back a changed name / controller, postfixes on RPC_SetTrigger and SetTrigger re-assert our controller and re-fire the carrier if anything swapped it inside. Valid for the routed frame only.
- Log: each distinct interception is written once as "[2HDW] trigger path: <what> - restored SBDW" (names the foreign controller / renamed trigger) - this is the in-game confirmation of the cause.
