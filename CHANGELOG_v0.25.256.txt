Aethelborn Ascended v0.25.256 - Whirlwind Arms   (base: v0.25.255; Anchor v0.25.254)

WHIRLWIND CA (Normal and Ascended) - the arms are now built by the mod itself, not adjusted
- At the final frame step the mod places BOTH arms with arm IK every frame, the same layout for every weapon, well out
  in front of the chest (elbows down and slightly out), then turns the wrists so each weapon's real tip points ahead, level.
- One weapon (any 1H or 2H: swords, axes, maces, battleaxes, atgeirs, spears...): right fist straight ahead of the chest
  at ~85% of arm reach, left hand on the handle just in front of it - the battleaxe hold, now for every weapon.
  2H swords no longer pull the arms in to the body.
- Two weapons (dual wield, Therzie dual models): both arms straight out side by side, each weapon pointing ahead.
- Spears (thrown spears only): head pointing out (as in v0.25.255) AND the spear now slides forward in the fist so the
  hand holds it near the butt, like an atgeir. Restored to the normal grip the moment the spin ends.
- Atgeirs: back to exactly how they were (v0.25.255's spear head check no longer touches them).
- Pause (Esc): arms held still (unchanged from v0.25.255).
- No damage, timing, VFX or sound change.

CHECKS: all ten modules compile (C# 5) against your real DLLs. Not tested in game.
