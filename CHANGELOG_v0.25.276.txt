Aethelborn Ascended v0.25.276 - Blue Title   (base: v0.25.275; ANCHOR v0.25.257)

MAIN MENU TITLE TAKES DEEP NORTH'S PLACE (your idea)
- The Aethelborn Ascended title now replaces Valheim's "Deep North" logo: the Deep North graphic is hidden and our title
  is placed on its spot (centred on it, a little wider than it, following it if the menu moves it).
- Blue like Deep North: when the menu loads, Deep North's picture is read back and its blue is measured; the gold, bronze
  and cream parts of our title are recoloured to that blue at the same brightness, so the design, bevels and shading
  stay. The dark letter fill and the blue gems are untouched. If the colour cannot be read, an icy blue is used.
- The top ornament (compass star and scrolls above AETHELBORN) is removed from AA_Title.png so it does not cover the
  Valheim logo above it; the star's spike through the first E was repaired. The bottom flourish stays. The image is now
  1600x502 (empty top trimmed).
- Turning [Main Menu] Use AA Main Menu off brings Deep North back.
- If Valheim's Deep North graphic cannot be found by name/picture/text, the title stays top-left (in blue) and the log
  lists the menu graphics it saw ("[AA Menu] Valheim's Deep North logo was not found ...") so it can be targeted exactly.
  When found, the log says "[AA Menu] title replaces Deep North (...)".

All v0.25.275 changes are included. No other changes.
CHECKS: all ten modules compile (C# 5) with the installer's reference set. The recolour preview was made offline with the
same algorithm (icy-blue fallback colour); the real Deep North blue is only read in game. Not tested in game.
