Aethelborn Ascended v0.25.172 - Parry Chain
Baseline: v0.25.171. Only the normal attack chain (NACC) changed.

NACC AFTER PARRY (user video: vanilla 1-2-3 after a parry)
- Likely cause (code): hit 3 of 1-2-1-2-3 steps BACK to swing 1 by cross-fading into an animator state the mod learned.
  Every animator controller change wiped ALL learned / captured swing states. A controller swap and swap-back around a
  block / parry (another animation mod, or our own dual-wield / paired controller restore) therefore left hit 3 with no
  state -> the native trigger ran -> vanilla chain look until the states were learned again.
- Fix: learned and captured swing states are now stored per controller and kept; a swap back to the same controller
  finds them again. A state is still only used under the controller it was learned on.
- Diagnostic: if the step back still finds nothing, LogOutput.log gets (max every 10 s):
  "[Aethelborn] NACC: no state for the step back to '...' (hit N, controller '...', blocking ...)".
- Unchanged: blocking / parry and their bonuses, heavy attacks, skill handoffs, 0.7 s window, timing. No delays.
