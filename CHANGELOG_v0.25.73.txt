Immortal Heroes v0.25.73 - Split Bars

ANIMATIONS
- Every animation is back to the v0.25.62 state, the one before the recent changes.
  - Reverted: Stomp, Olympic Hero / jump slams (Smite, Bonecrusher), the Circle Swing crow hop and the landing kneel.
- Kept: the recast cancel for Whirlwind, Furious Winds and Starfall Volley, and every VFX.

HUD
- Stamina and Eitr now share one bar line, split into two halves.
  - Stamina is the yellow left half, Eitr the purple right half, with a painted divider in the middle.
  - The old Eitr row is cut out of all four class frames, so the HUD is one row shorter. EXP and food move up.
- The numbers are bigger, with a black outline that stays readable on any bar colour.
  - Warrior: the value column shows HP, then "Stamina | Eitr" in yellow and purple, then your level.
  - Cleric, Sorcerer and Ranger: each bar or half shows its number in the middle, and the level is on the EXP bar.

VFX - STATUS VISUALS (all classes)
- Stun: golden stars circle over the head.
- Frost: an icy rime coat with drifting snow for as long as it lasts.
  - Freeze also cracks out an ice shell with a flash.
- Expose: crimson shards fall off the chest while it lasts.
- Cripple: a dark violet haze around the feet.
- Fire Burn / Spirit Burn: each tick licks flames (fire orange or holy white-gold) off the target.
- Judgement Mark: a rose-gold sigil hovers over the head, with a spinning ground ring.
- Sanctified ally: emerald motes rise around them.
- Hyper Armor: a golden shimmer on you while it lasts.
- Re-applying an effect never stacks extra visuals, and everything turns off with [Visuals] EnhancedSkillVfx.
