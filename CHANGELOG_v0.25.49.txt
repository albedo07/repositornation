Immortal Heroes v0.25.49 - Ranger Rework

ACROBAT
- Ricochet Arrow -> SOMERSAULT DANCE: front flip 3m up onto a spot within 5m (always 0.5s), foot slam
  (260% Blunt, 4m, Stuns Small + Big), backflip 2m up / 2m away (0.5s). Ascended: the backflip goes 3m up,
  you loose a volley of three 3m circles side by side (OOO) at your aim, then fall normally (no fall damage).
- Cyclone Arrow: travels slower (30m in 9s). Ascended: a tall tornado 2m wider that sucks Small and Big
  enemies in while it moves and Frosts everything it hits, Bosses included (replaces the split).
- Skyfall Barrage: 10 shots. Ascended: 12 shots, then a Blunt slam straight down (6m, Stuns Small + Big,
  Cripples Bosses) and a 2m backflip; 2 charges (each on the normal cooldown).
- Gale Volley Ascended: 3 charges (second fan kept).
- Tailwind: +60% Move Speed and every movement penalty removed (encumbrance stays).

BOWMASTER (much more damage)
- Ballista 300% (+60%/stack), Arrow Rain 40%/hit, Pinning 240%, Explosive 320%, Starfall 65%/hit + 450% final.
- Arrow Rain: 2 charges (Ascended 3).
- Pinning Shot Ascended: pinned enemies are Exposed while pinned, then explode (3m, 180%) and stay Exposed 6s.
- Splitting Arrow: 2 spread shots 0.5s apart (25m), then press again within 3s for a great 4m-wide piercing
  arrow (380%). Ascended: 3 spread shots, and after the great arrow a giant sky arrow falls at your aim
  (4m, Blunt + Pierce, Stuns every enemy, Bosses included).
- Explosive Arrow: 30m shot (320%), then 0.5s later cluster arrow bombs land on the blast: three 3m circles
  side by side. Ascended: hold up to 3s, +1 explosive shot per second (re-aimed as each is loosed), +25%
  damage per stack; clusters are 4m, land 1s after their blast and leave burning ground.

FIXES
- Raised-arm buff animation (Tailwind, Hawk's Vigil and every other buff raise) is faster and stops the
  moment you move after the cast - no more gliding with the arm up. Every emote a skill starts is stopped
  when its animation ends.
- Mercenary: Unchained Fury now makes EVERY Mercenary skill its Ascended version while it lasts.
- Seismic Guillotine Ascended: 3 fissures (was only during Fury).
