Immortal Heroes v0.19.1 - Universal Tree

Priest tree now uses the exact same panel as Paladin
- The Priest painting was generated separately and drifts up to 3 px from the Paladin art
  (panel border, nodes, Grace box). v0.19.0 copied its whole right panel at runtime, which
  left seams and shifted every Priest node, nameplate, star row and lock.
- New pipeline (tools/build_priest_assets.py): each Priest node, the PRIEST title and the
  Grand Sigil box are cut from the painting, auto-aligned to the matching Paladin node slot
  and placed on the Paladin backdrop. Frame, header, footer, background and every node slot
  are now pixel-identical between Paladin and Priest.
- New files: Cleric_Priest_Reference.png, Cleric_Priest_Locked.png (greyed exactly like
  Paladin's locked nodes), Icon_<priest skill>.png hotbar icons built with the same slot frames
  as the Paladin icons. The source painting moved to docs/source_art (not shipped).
- Priest node rects now equal the Paladin slot rects (click areas, padlocks, stars, anchors).
- PALADIN / PRIEST preview selector uses the ornate plaque art instead of flat boxes.

Design preview only (not in game yet)
- docs/previews/ALTAR_ClassSelect_*.png: the Dragon's Altar Class selection on the same
  universal panel (Classes as nodes on the left, focused Class details on the right,
  hotbar preview + CHOOSE plaque in the footer).
