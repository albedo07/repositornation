Aethelborn Ascended v0.25.264 - Main Menu   (base: ANCHOR v0.25.257 + v0.25.258-263)

MAIN MENU (nothing else in Valheim's menu is changed)
- The Aethelborn Ascended title sits right under Valheim's logo (80% of the logo's width, centred). It is part of the
  main menu itself, so it hides with it when you open character select, settings and other screens.
- MenuMusic plays on the main menu: the whole 30 s track, looping forever (also when Valheim's "continuous music" is
  off). It runs through Valheim's own music system, so the Music volume slider and fades apply.
- NEW SETTING [Main Menu] Use AA Main Menu = true. false = the standard vanilla main menu (title hidden, vanilla music
  back). Applies live (F8 > General, or the .cfg).
- Log lines "[AA Menu] title placed ..." and "[AA Menu] menu music ..." confirm both on start-up.

MOD ICON
- icon.png (256x256) of the AA crest is included at the top of the package (mod managers / Thunderstore use it).

NEW FILES: ImmortalHeroesAssets\AA_Title.png, ImmortalHeroesAssets\SFX\MenuMusic.wav (decoded from your MP3 unchanged),
icon.png. The installer copies them with the other assets.
No skill, combat, VFX or sound-mix changes.

CHECKS: all ten modules compile (C# 5) against your real DLLs. Not tested in game.
