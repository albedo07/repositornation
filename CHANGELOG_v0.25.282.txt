AETHELBORN ASCENDED v0.25.282 - Icons and Banners
=================================================

PRIEST - Shield Mastery (Holy Parry / Holy Shockwave)
- Holy Shockwave cooldown 15 s -> 20 s (new config key Cooldown_v025282).
- Skill enhancement from a parry: +35% -> +50% of the skill's damage for the WHOLE skill instance
  (e.g. a Lightning Relic pulse of 50 becomes 75 for every pulse). New key NextSkillDamagePercent_v025282.
- Enhancement cooldown 25 s -> 30 s, starting when the enhanced skill uses it (new key EmpowerCooldown_v025282).
- Icons: "Holy Parry - Ready" and "Holy Parry - Active" removed.
  * Holy Shockwave: ONE icon that switches between Ready (gold rings) and Cooldown (dark, 20 s).
  * Skill Enhanced (blue sword + gold sparkle) while the next skill is boosted.
  * Holy Parry - Cooldown (dark buckler) during the 30 s recharge.
  * The parry's Hyper Armor shows the Hyper Armor icon (chestplate).
- Mastery text updated (+50% for the whole skill, 30 s, 20 s).

PRIEST - Heaven's Judgement (Ascended only)
- The final big beam now damages the ENTIRE skill radius (was only the small pillar radius).
  Its gold ring now expands over the whole area so you can see it. Normal version unchanged.

ENEMY DEBUFF ICONS (new)
- Small diamond icons above enemy health bars: Poisoned, Burning, Frost, Crippled, Exposed, Zapped, Stunned.
  Read from Valheim's enemy HUD every 0.2 s; drawing only in OnGUI. If the HUD layout cannot be read the feature
  turns itself off once and logs "[AA] Enemy debuff icons disabled".

GROUND FIRE
- Fader-like lingering fire, but shallow (ankle to knee): many small low tongues (size 0.38-0.7 instead of up to 4),
  slower rise, muted orange-red flames, a darker glowing ember bed, low dark rolling smoke, half the sparks.
  Same anchor cap (180) and particle caps -> same or lower cost. Used by every fire field (Bomb, Crescent trail,
  Meteor, Ranger fire field, Flame Burst, etc.).

FURY / OVERCHARGE BANNER (new)
- While Unchained Fury (Mercenary) or Overcharge (Wizard) is active: a bold glowing title over the hotbar
  ("UNCHAINED FURY 14s" fire red-orange / "OVERCHARGE 9s" arcane violet), a pulsing frame around the hotbar,
  a time-left bar, and a flash + pop when it starts. Replaces the small blue Fury title while Fury is active.
