NEW: weapon aiming for non-vanilla weapons (user: Therzie's weapons did not point outwards in Whirlwind)
- Every skill pose that aims the held weapon (Whirlwind / Ascended Whirlwind spin, Circle Swing, other spins and swings)
  finds the weapon's tip on the weapon's own long axis, using only the mesh in that hand. Before, it took the centre of
  the whole item's bounding box: wrong for models built along another axis or with extra parts (a dual weapon's second
  blade in the other hand, sheaths, trails), so the blade hung down.
- Vanilla weapons: same result (tip on the blade axis); centrally held items (bows, staves) keep their vanilla angle.
- If a model is unusual, the old rule is used as a fallback.
Limit: a Therzie dual weapon is ONE item; its second blade is part of the model and moves with it as Therzie built it.
