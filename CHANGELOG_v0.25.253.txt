Aethelborn Ascended v0.25.253 - Final Frame Aim   (base: v0.25.252; Anchor v0.25.244)

GOAL (agreed): during Whirlwind (Normal and Ascended), standing or moving, every weapon (vanilla, Therzie, 1H, 2H, dual):
arms straight out front at shoulder height, hands on the grip, each blade level and pointing straight ahead.

WHY IT FLICKERED (up / ahead / up)
- The blade tips were measured correctly (v0.25.252 log: 1.68 m, 1.17 m, 0.60 m), but the aim was applied in a script
  step whose "run last" order Unity does not guarantee for mods loaded at runtime. On some frames the skill animation's
  own hand code ran after it and rewrote the hands. The old "0 deg" log line measured right after our own aim, so it
  could never show this.

WHIRLWIND CA (Normal and Ascended)
- The final aim now runs in a step inserted into Unity's own frame loop, right before Unity updates the renderers and
  skinned meshes - after every script of every mod. Nothing can rewrite the hands between it and the drawn frame.
  (The earlier aim is kept too; if the hook cannot be installed, the log says so.)
- Honest measurement: at that final step the blade angle is measured BEFORE the final aim, so the log shows how often
  something had moved the hands after us.
- Log lines:
  "Whirlwind blade: frame hook installed before UpdateAllRenderers" (once per session)
  "Whirlwind blade: <item> right tip X m ..." (per weapon)
  "Whirlwind blade result: N frames, hands overwritten after our step on M frames, worst angle ..." (per spin)
- No damage, timing, VFX or sound change.

CHECKS: all ten modules compile (C# 5) against your real DLLs. Not tested in game.
