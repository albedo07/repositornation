v0.25.149 Pull Remainder
- Crescent pull (multiplayer): SendCleavePull sends at most 0.75 m and keeps the remainder for the next 0.1 s flush; UpdateCleavePullNet applies at most 0.75 m per frame and keeps the remainder for the next frame. No movement is discarded.
- Pending movement (out + inbox) is cleared for dead, destroyed or invalid targets. Obstacle checks (CleavePullStep) unchanged.
