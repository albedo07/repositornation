Aethelborn Ascended v0.25.246 - Whirlwind Weapon Tips   (base: v0.25.245; Anchor v0.25.244)

WHIRLWIND (Mercenary, Normal and Ascended) - and every skill pose that aims a held weapon
- Cause (from your videos): vanilla weapons already pointed out; Therzie swords (2H, 2H dual, dual curved) pointed straight up.
  Their tip was measured wrongly: skinned weapon meshes report their box in another space, so "tip" landed beside the grip.
- Weapon tip is now measured from the weapon mesh's own bounds (skinned meshes included), then cross-checked against the
  far end of its world box; if the two disagree by more than 55 degrees the far end wins. One universal rule for vanilla and Therzie.
- The log now prints one line per weapon: "[Aethelborn] Weapon tip <item> / <mesh> (<type>): (x, y, z)". Send it if any weapon is still off.
- No damage, timing, VFX or sound change. v0.25.245 sound defaults and HUD fixes kept.

CHECKS: all ten modules compile (C# 5) against your real DLLs. Not tested in game.
