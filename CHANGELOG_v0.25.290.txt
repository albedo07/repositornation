AETHELBORN ASCENDED v0.25.290 - Horizon Walker Pass   (Sorcerer bloodline only; Warrior and Cleric untouched)

HORIZON WALKER
- Stonefang Eruption (Ascended): ONE eruption. The spikes rise once and stay up for 8 s (no repeated spikes). Every
  0.3 s, an enemy inside the field that moved (or just walked in) takes 12% damage; enemies standing still take none.
  Config: SpikeDuration_v025290 (8 s), SpikeMoveTickPercent_v025290 (12%). The tooltip states the new rule.
- Void Step: the teleport sound (DBZ) follows you instead of staying at the start point.
- Arcane Phantom / Rift Echo: they now fire with every real attack of the weapon you hold, so they copy its attack
  speed (Staff of Embers is no longer a machine gun; Gun Staff and other staves fire at their own rates). For
  burst weapons, each burst shot is mirrored at the weapon's own burst interval. (EchoInterval_v0234 is no longer used.)
- Arcane Phantom (Normal): each phantom has its own buff icon with its number in the bottom-left corner
  (1, 2, 3) and its own timer. Conjuring a new phantom no longer resets the icon of the existing one.
  Ascended twins keep the single Arcane Phantom icon.
- Arcane Phalanx: summon = your new MagicSword recording (leading silence trimmed, level matched to the
  other sword sounds). Ascended spears = MagicSword3 at normal pitch (the old pitched-down reuse is gone).
- Arcane Rupture: new wind-up sound. The void swirl (BlackHole2, raised to 120% pitch) fades in at the target
  during the wind-up and hands over to BoomBass + SolidBoom with a short overlap. New slider:
  Spellcaster Arcane Rupture Audio > Wind Up Volume (%).
- Rift Walker:
  * Portal A closes if Portal B is not placed within 5 s (PlacementWindow_v025290 = 5).
  * Portal look: one flat disc (no tunnel cones or double rim, so it no longer looks like a yoyo from the side),
    with no purple glow in the middle. It is a violet vortex with spinning spiral streaks, a bright rim and small
    crackling lightning arcs along the edge.
  * Sound: the drop before the hum loop is fixed. Cause: the recording faded out while the loop faded in, both on
    the same -30 dB curve, so halfway through both were near -18 dB (a ~15 dB hole). Now the hum reaches full level
    first (4.2-4.8 s), and only then does the recording fade out (4.8-5.5 s).
- Rift Echo: the purple outline rebuilds whenever your visible gear changes. It now follows the weapon you hold, and
  a holstered weapon is outlined on your back as it actually sits (no leftover cast-time weapon).
