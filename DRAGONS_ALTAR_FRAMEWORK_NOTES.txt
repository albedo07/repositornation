IMMORTAL HEROES - v0.13.1 IMPLEMENTATION NOTES

FOUNDATION
- Cleric now owns two explicit Weapon Mastery Blessings: Shield Weapon Mastery and Divine Duality. The old hidden blanket equipment movement-speed exemption is retired.
- Sword Master, Mercenary, Wizard and Spellcaster mastery rules are runtime equipment-state checks rather than permanent unconditional buffs.
- Paladin passive selection is stored as one locked choice and cleared by Class Reset.
- Arcane Blood Magic Damage is defined as an umbrella for Eitr-based magic, including magical physical portions and magic-weapon attacks.
- Sorcerer's emergency teleport is documented only; no unsafe automatic teleport code is included yet.

PRIEST
- Grand Cross is self-cast only. Divine Intervention and Heaven's Judgement retain Cross Cast support.
- Grand Sigil Armor bonus scales from current Armor rather than adding flat Armor.
- Grand Sigil death-save supports nearby allies without replacing the Priest's existing self-save or active barrier.

DEFERRED
- Weapon-combination-specific animations.
- #2 regressions: Elemental Cataclysm charge, Astral Greatblade crosshair, Arcane Phalanx anti-spam, 5th-hit indicator removal.
- Sword Master and Paladin skill-identity rewrites.


UI ASSET PASS
- Cleric -> Paladin Skill Tree now prefers a cached external reference-quality PNG from BepInEx/plugins/ImmortalHeroesAssets.
- The procedural renderer remains fallback-only.
- Color Key is masked out in the live reference render.
