IMMORTAL HEROES v0.25.15 - VANGUARD AND OLYMPIC HERO (Animation Blueprint, part A)

Engine (from the blueprint audit):
- LEGS: skill clips now move the legs too (Unity humanoid muscles: thigh lift, spread, knee bend, foot), applied on
  the animator's real pose with the same no-stacking rule as the arms. [Runtime] LegMotionScale_v02515
  (1 = normal, 0 = legs untouched, -1 = inverted if knees bend the wrong way).
- FORCED RUN: skills that move the body themselves (Shield Charge, Frenzied Charge) now play Valheim's real
  running legs while they move.
- Release-first: instant casts react after 0.05 s (was 0.1 s minimum).
- Held clips end on their gameplay event (landing, bash, release); the old 12 s cap is gone (60 s safety only).
- Automatic procs (Unchained Fury roar, Overcharge, Bless Thy Sinners rise, Holy Shockwave) are low-priority
  accents: they never replace a skill animation that is playing.
- Axial roll: clips can spin the body around its own head-to-feet axis.

Clips:
- SHIELD CHARGE (Vanguard, shield profile): brace low behind the shield, then a real run with the torso leaned in,
  shield leading at chest height and the weapon trailing low behind. The Bash is a shoulder-first shield shove
  with lunge legs, then you recover. Steering, collision and distance are unchanged.
- FRENZIED CHARGE (Vanguard, thrust profile): braced lunge with the blade drawn back, then the thrust leads a
  running dash.
- ELECTRIC SMITE / BONECRUSHER (Olympic Hero): crouch load, launch almost horizontal, one full barrel roll over
  the ascent/apex, unwind (left foot forward, fist cocked). The poised fall holds through any descent and the
  superhero landing (fist on the ground, left foot planted, right knee folded behind, head up) plays on the real
  ground contact, synced with the damage. The main weapon is hidden render-only while the fist is down (nothing
  is unequipped). Bonecrusher = deeper load and a heavier settle.

Docs: the animation blueprint is stored in docs/reference/animation_blueprint_v02514.
