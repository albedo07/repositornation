Aethelborn Ascended v0.25.269 - Storm Sounds and Strike Sync   (base: v0.25.268; ANCHOR v0.25.257)

MAIN MENU MUSIC
- Starting a game now fades the music out (3 s) from the moment Valheim shows its loading screen, instead of cutting.
  The fade counts real frames, so a long loading frame can no longer skip it. Log: "[AA Menu] music fading out" /
  "[AA Menu] music faded out".

RIGHTEOUS STRIKE (Ascended) RIGHT AFTER GODDESS RELIC - CA OUT OF SYNC (your video)
- Both skills borrow Valheim's hammer swing (swing_sledge). When Righteous Strike was cast while Goddess Relic's swing
  was still finishing, the new swing was not restarted: the animation waited for the old swing to end and then played
  at its natural speed, so the lightning landed while you were still swinging. The mod now restarts the swing from its
  first frame in that case and times it to the strike, also after the animator was rebuilt.
- Applies to every skill pair that borrows the same Valheim swing back to back (the Relics, Heaven's Judgement,
  Righteous Strike - Normal and Ascended).
- Log lines "CA sync: '<swing>' was still playing ... restarted" / "... was not followed" show what happened.

MERCENARY / PALADIN
- Bonecrusher (Normal and Ascended): BoulderCrash added on landing (with HeavyPunch + BassDrop).
- Angel Comet (Normal and Ascended): BoulderCrash added on landing (with HeavyPunch + BassDrop).

PRIEST
- Lightning Tempest (Normal and Ascended): LightningStorm (your new recording) plays over the whole storm, growing in over
  0.8 s and fading out over 1.5 s after the last strike; LightningCrash (the Righteous Strike sound) on every lightning
  strike. Ascended: MidBoom as each meteor lands. New audio section: Priest Lightning Tempest Audio (Skill Volume +
  Lightning Storm / Lightning Crash / Meteor Boom sliders).
- Heaven's Judgement (Normal and Ascended): the Judgement sound now grows in gradually over the whole wind-up and fades
  out gradually (1.5 s) after the skill ends - no instant start or stop.
