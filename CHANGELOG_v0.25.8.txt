IMMORTAL HEROES v0.25.8 - WARRIOR MOTIONS (animation pass 2 of 4) + HUD LAYOUT

HUD
- Skill hotbar: default = bottom-left corner (no more overlap with the hover text / crosshair area).
  Draggable anywhere while the inventory is open ([Immortal HUD] SkillBarPosX / SkillBarPosY, -1 = default).
- Stat HUD: default = right above the skill hotbar (bottom-left, where the vanilla vitals were). Still draggable.
- Stat HUD is thinner: HP / STA + EIT / Lv + EXP, then the vanilla food in ONE horizontal row under the EXP bar.
- Both HUDs stay visible while the inventory is open (so you can move them).
- Buffs with a number (Barrier HP) now show the number on the icon AND the timer under it.

PRIEST
- Ascended Lightning Tempest no longer follows you: it stays where you cast it (12m), like the normal one.

ANIMATION ENGINE
- Chained clips continue from the current pose (no snap to rest between combo hits); spins at constant speed.

WARRIOR / SWORD MASTER / MERCENARY (every skill, incl. Ascended)
- Heavy Slash: weapon over the right shoulder, crushing diagonal cut.  Ascended (Merc): wound back at the waist, huge horizontal sweep.
- Impact Wave (+ SM Ascended): crouch with the blade dragged low, rising slash.
- Impact Punch: fist chambered at the hip, full-body straight punch.
- Moonlight Splitter: low ready stance, then alternating right/left cuts per wave; Ascended finisher = two-handed sky-to-ground cut.
- Crescent Cleave: blade pulled low behind the right hip, wide sweep to the left (no spin).
- Blade Storm: hand on the hilt, lightning draw, sheath.
- Frenzied Charge: drawn-back thrust stance, lunging dash.
- Eclipse: blade raised before the face, full spin slash.
- Halfmoon: long trembling pull, wide release, backhand second slash (no spin). Ascended: stance held until
  Left Click, spinning finisher; no click = smooth return.
- Knight's Guidance: salute, blade thrust to the sky.
- Stomp: lift, crash, two aftershock bounces.
- Circle Swing: weapon wound back while you walk, full spin swing (Ascended second spin).
- Bonecrusher: weapon overhead through the jump, crash landing (Electric Smite uses the same air pose).
- Seismic Guillotine: overhead chop into the ground.
- Punishing Bomb: two-handed bat swing.
- Whirlwind: arms out, constant spin for the whole duration (Ascended faster + final sweep).
- Battlecry / Unchained Fury: chest-out roar with a shake.
