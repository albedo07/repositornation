Immortal Heroes v0.18.1 - Functional Skill Tree (Paladin)

Hotbar
- Cleric (before Advancement) and Paladin now cast from the Skill Tree hotbar: each numbered slot
  casts the skill placed there in the tree, using the key you bound under it (default M4 + 1..7).
- Grace slot: M4 + R casts Heaven's Light.
- The old fixed [Hotkeys] no longer cast Cleric / Paladin skills (other classes unchanged).
- New in-game HUD for Cleric / Paladin: the same icons and order as the tree hotbar,
  key under each slot, cooldown on the icon, Grace slot on the right, level in the title.
- Before Advancement the bar starts with Lightning Zap, Righteous Strike, Holy Wave.

Heaven's Light (Grace) - now a real skill
- You and players within 10m: +40% Overall Defense and no equipment movement penalties for 60s.
- 10 min cooldown, free, 0.5s instant cast. Re-casting refreshes, never stacks. [Paladin Heavens Light]

Advance / Ascend from the tree
- Before Advancement the Paladin panel shows an ADVANCE TO PALADIN plaque with the checklist:
  Lv 16, Righteous Strike Tier 7, all 14 Class Tier Points spent.
- The Altar now refuses Paladin until the same requirements are met (and says what is missing).
- Select an eligible skill: an ASCEND plaque appears where CONFIRM goes (click twice to confirm).
  Tooltips say when each skill can Ascend.

Polish
- Tier stars are centered under the nameplate; the n/max count sits to their right.
- Tooltip keywords are colored like vanilla: numbers in orange, damage types by element
  (Fire, Frost, Lightning, Poison, Spirit, physical), effects (Expose, Cripple, Stun, Zap,
  Judgement Mark...) in violet, buffs / HP in green, Stamina in yellow.
