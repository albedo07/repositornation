Aethelborn Ascended v0.25.270 - Charge Whoosh and Strike Timing   (base: v0.25.269; ANCHOR v0.25.257)

RIGHTEOUS STRIKE RIGHT AFTER GODDESS RELIC - STILL LATE (root cause found)
- Your log shows that the v0.25.269 restart did happen ("CA sync: 'swing_sledge' was still playing ... restarted"), so
  the restart was not the problem. The real problem: a restart cross-fades the hammer swing into ITSELF. During that
  cross-fade both the old swing (Goddess Relic's, nearly finished) and the new one have the same animation state, and the
  speed code read the OLD one. Its late frame made the new swing look almost done, so the whole new swing was given a
  very slow speed (about 0.3x) and the hammer came down long after the lightning.
- Fix: the speed is now worked out from the NEW swing only (the swing being faded in), and nothing is timed until the
  restart has really begun. This also corrects every skill whose animation starts part-way in (Start % above 0, e.g.
  Righteous Strike starts at 40%), since that jump is the same kind of self cross-fade.
- Affects: Righteous Strike (Normal and Ascended), Goddess Relic, Lightning Relic, Holy Relic, Heaven's Judgement and any
  other skill that borrows a Valheim swing with a Start % or back to back. Damage and skill timing are unchanged; only
  the animation speed now follows the right swing.
- New log line (first 3 per animation, plus 3 restarted): "CA timing <animation> #n: the swing's hit frame came X s
  AFTER/before the skill's hit". This measures the visible result. Please send it if anything still looks late.

SHIELD CHARGE (Paladin) - BIGWHOOSH (your request)
- Normal and Ascended: BigWhoosh now builds up during the charge. It is slowed to 60% speed (time-stretched, pitch kept)
  so its rise covers the charge, and it is timed so its climax lands on the Hammer Slam of a full-length charge
  (Normal: end of the charge + the instant slam; Ascended: end of the charge + the jump to the slam).
- Slam earlier (Left Click, or a big enemy stops you): the build-up fades out and the BigWhoosh climax plays exactly on
  that slam instead.
- Only up to the climax: the build-up fades as the charge ends; the climax copy plays at 85% length and fades out after its
  peak, so you do not hear the whole recording.
- Existing footsteps, MidBoom and LightningCrash on the slam are unchanged.
- New sliders under Paladin Shield Charge Audio: Charge Whoosh Build-up Volume (%) and Charge Whoosh Climax Volume (%),
  each with its own Pitch / Speed / Length / Bass settings in F8.

MAIN MENU MUSIC (your request)
- The music fades out (2.5 s curve) when you click Play, and as soon as it is quiet (-20 dB) it stops for good. It no
  longer keeps playing quietly through the loading screen. The fade runs on real time; a loading hitch counts at most
  0.25 s, so it can never jump from loud to off.
