Aethelborn Ascended v0.25.249 - True Weapon Tip   (base: v0.25.248; Anchor v0.25.244)

WHIRLWIND CA (Normal and Ascended) and every skill pose that aims a held weapon
- The weapon tip is now the real point of the weapon model farthest from the hand (every vertex is checked; skinned
  Therzie meshes are baked in their pose first). Before, it was the end of a box drawn around the model, which is off
  for curved/angled blades - that is why Therzie blades pointed up.
- Every frame the hand turns so that exact point lies straight ahead, level (Whirlwind: both hands on the weapon,
  arms forward at shoulder height). Same for vanilla, Therzie and any other mod's weapons.
- Bows/staves held in the middle keep the vanilla angle. Weapons whose model cannot be read fall back to the old rule
  (+ v0.25.248 learned direction for Therzie). Measured once per equipped weapon - no combat cost.
- Log: "[Aethelborn] Weapon tip <item>: (x, y, z)" once per weapon.
- No damage, timing, VFX or sound change.

CHECKS: all ten modules compile (C# 5) against your real DLLs. Not tested in game.
