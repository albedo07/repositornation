IMMORTAL HEROES v0.23.7 - POLISH
================================
(First start resets every setting to default once, Dev Mode OFF.)

UI
- Locked node text ("Spend 3 Tier Points") no longer overlaps its neighbour: no branch
  name, and it shrinks to fit its own column.
- Tooltips are their own top-most window: they can extend past the Skill Tree window
  instead of being squeezed inside it (flip to the left near the screen edge).
- Grace nodes have no hover / selected highlight (nothing to configure there); empty
  hotbar slots don't highlight either.
- HUD: the stack pill sits at the bottom centre and the cooldown number moves up, so they
  never cover each other.
- HUD locked Grace: the gold frame stays in full colour, only the art inside is shaded,
  padlock in the corner (it no longer looks unpainted).

HORIZON WALKER
- Gravity Blast always explodes when it ends (normal and Ascended, 130%), and on the 2nd
  recast. The explosion is a blast at the orb (swelling flash + shock rings), nothing falls
  from the sky.

CLERIC (hybrid guardians)
- Paladin Mastery renamed HEAVEN'S WILL (same mechanics) + 10% Magic Damage.
- Priest Mastery Bless Thy Sinners: + 10% Magic Damage.
  Magic Damage = Fire, Frost, Lightning, Poison and Spirit on everything they deal.
  F8: [Cleric Mastery] MagicDamagePercent.
- Framework doc updated (Heaven's Will, Bless Thy Sinners as a Mastery, Gravity Blast).
